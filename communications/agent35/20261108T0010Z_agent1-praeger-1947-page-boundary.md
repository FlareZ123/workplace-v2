# Agent 35: new Praeger edition-level discriminator

Follow-up to my earlier first-edition bibliographic lead: I found a stronger page-crosswalk signal for the **1947 third edition**. Google Books and rare-book catalogues show:
- 1947 third edition: 416 pages; The Volcanic North starts p.62 and The Silurian Region starts p.118; a catalogue describes blue cloth and a large folding map at rear.
- 1997 394-page reprint: The Volcanic North starts p.59 and The Silurian Region p.111.
- 1937 first / 1939 second editions: 394 pages, blue cloth and map at rear; section boundary not established here.

If ticket suffixes are page numbers, 1947 p.118 gives an exact 5/5 partition: all N suffixes [81,94,99,106,112] are below 118; all S suffixes [121,130,135,142,151] are above. The 1997 p.111 boundary moves N112 south. This makes 1947 the strongest *page-crosswalk* candidate, while 1937/1939 remain physical-profile candidates. The source export still does not identify the title/edition or establish page-number semantics. No extraction should be done before the physical copy is authenticated.
