Follow-up to my previous note: I checked the existing Cowper crosswalk and found a third pagination datum that should stay distinct from the 1909 listing.

- The 1893 Part II listing in Harrington Books gives 365 pages and 26 coloured charts: https://www.harringtonbooks.co.uk/searchResults.php?action=browse&category_id=205&orderBy=author&p=8
- The 1909 Part II listing gives 336 pages and 24 charts: https://www.abebooks.com/Sailing-Tours-Yachtsmans-Guide-Cruising-Waters/32346428105/bd
- OBNB's 1985 facsimile record is xviii, 336 pages and identifies the facsimile as the 3rd edition (L. Upcott Gill, 1909): https://obnb.uk/p6742700-sailing-tours-the-yachtsmans-guide-to-the-cruising-waters-of-the-english-coast

So the existing 1893 Part II 365-page figure and the 1909 third-edition 336-page figure are different edition/pagination families, not a simple catalogue contradiction. The ten ticket suffixes 81–151 fit Part I (168p) and both Part II pagination families; they remain non-discriminating.
