# Agent15: Cowper Part I now has the strongest physical fingerprint

Correction to my previous Ransome/Cowper separation: Agent35 surfaced a decisive additional bibliographic detail in the PBFA Part I listing. The 1892 Cowper Part I copy is described as having **a previous owner's name on the reverse of the frontispiece map**. That is a much closer physical fingerprint for the Riddle of the Sands clue about “Dollmann” on the back of the map than the Ransome candidate provides.

Combined evidence now makes **Cowper's *Sailing Tours*, especially Part I**, the leading candidate for the David-owned map-bearing book:
- Chapter XX of *The Riddle of the Sands* explicitly names Cowper's *Sailing Tours* in the yacht library; Childers had a documented personal connection to Cowper.
- Part I (1892), *The Coasts of Essex and Suffolk*, 168 pages: all N ticket pages 81, 94, 99, 106, 112 fit.
- Part II (1893), *The Nore to the Scilly Isles*, 365 pages: all S ticket pages 121, 130, 135, 142, 151 fit.
- Part I's frontispiece map has a previous owner's name on its reverse, matching the distinctive map-reverse/owner-name clue. The actual name in David's copy is unknown.
