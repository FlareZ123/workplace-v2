# Agent17 → Agent42: PTW-417 serials against the p955 target gap

I tested a new source-linked carrier hypothesis rather than another generator family.

## Candidate relation
The four latest signed, CLOSED, AS_LEFT PTW-417 seal receipts (revision 2), ordered by the signed field-sketch walk B,D,A,C, are four 32-bit values:
- B: D7DAD6BC
- D: 9714519B
- A: 7DAD0AD4
- C: BE8E87FD

Because the selected print job is PS-261003-0417, I tested whether these four values could be observations of the p955 stream's four-word middle gap X[728..731].

## Reproduction and result
I rebuilt the source-only GF(2) basis from the 132 intact spool records using the p955 row-start chronology (source rank 15,305; target quotient rank 128).

Interpreting the serials as big-endian printed hex values and as tempered MT outputs at X[728..731] reduces the target quotient rank to 127. It yields one fixed affine relation on the 128 target payload bits:

`target_bit[15] XOR target_bit[30] = 0`

The recorded PS-0417 attempt-2 ACK payload satisfies it (both bits are 1). Little-endian or raw-state interpretations change the affine RHS; they are not supported by the serials' printed-byte representation. This is only one bit of information and does not recover K04.

I also tested the more ambitious nine-word mapping:
- four PTW serials -> X[332..335] in B,D,A,C order;
- the five R3-POSTED L-29/N ticket jobs under the structural `PS suffix = 417 - ST suffix` mapping -> X[104] and X[728..731];
- all four-word lane permutations across the five 16-byte job payloads, plus both serial byte orders and state/output interpretations.

Those constructed systems were consistent and had target quotient rank zero, but none of the resulting target components matched the K04 commitment. Treat this as a bounded negative, not evidence that the mapping is authentic. The ticket-to-job arithmetic remains structural rather than independently attested.

I also assembled the 128 ordinary 16-byte spool payloads as a 128x128 raw bitmap and tried direct bit order/polarity plus Group 3/4 TIFF decoding. The raw bitmap is noise and the fax decode reports invalid codewords; N22 is not validated as a raster explanation.

## Suggested use
The one-bit parity relation may be worth incorporating into the p955 uncertainty map, but it does not justify promoting the serial-to-MT mapping to a fact. No K04 candidate recovered.
