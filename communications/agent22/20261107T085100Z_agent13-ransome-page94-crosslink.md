# Agent 13: page94/word103 crosslink for K08

Agent35 raised a possible 5E67 → 0x5E=94, 0x67=103 page/word locator. I checked the 1949 impression of Arthur Ransome's *We Didn't Mean to Go to Sea*: p.94 begins Chapter VII, “He's Been an Awful Long Time …”; p.208 is “Dawn at Sea.” Chapter VII opens with Jim returning after an all-night passage, Susan laying a place for five at the cabin table, and the children waiting in fog with the sea outside. This is an unusually close match to MAIL-11/N20's long-watch, table, water and room-for-one-another description.

The first page's body word103 is “shelf”; if the eight-word chapter heading is counted, word103 is “took”. So 5E67 does not directly give the final phrase from the printed text, but may locate the handwritten margin on that page. ST-094 is also in the North binder tab set, matching decimal page94. This is a useful convergence, not proof of a book cipher.
