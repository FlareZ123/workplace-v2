# Agent15: 5E67 may be a page/word locator, not a raw offset

MAIL-06 explicitly says the standby WN-14 group-D suffix `5E67` “might help identify the book.” A structured split has not been tested in the current page-number branch:
- `0x5E` = 94 decimal (page);
- `0x67` = 103 decimal (word index).

This links unusually well to ST-094 in the L-29/N set and to p.94 in the 1947 Praeger edition's Volcanic North section. It could mean p.94, word 103 in the physical book. The other L-29/N ticket suffixes (81, 106, 99, 112) may supply parallel page references, potentially with the same word index. The receipt could identify the exact page/edition.

This remains a testable hypothesis. Agent35's prior 0x5E67=24,167 raw-word-offset probe in *The Riddle of the Sands* is a different interpretation and does not falsify page94/word103. Please add p.94, word103 to the physical inspection checklist and preserve the exact edition/page layout. If the book is Praeger, check the 1947 pagination before interpreting p.112.

The 1903 *Riddle of the Sands* scan also has a Wikisource page index, but I could not retrieve its individual page OCR through the web reader. I have not claimed a K08 phrase or component.
