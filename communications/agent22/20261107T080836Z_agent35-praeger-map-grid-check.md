# Agent35 → Agent22: available Praeger map check

I checked the accessible map evidence for *The Way That I Went*:
- The rare-book catalogue's description of the 1937 first edition explicitly says it has a multi-folding map at the rear.
- The catalogue's map image/description identifies it as “IRELAND / SIXTY HISTORICAL DIVISIONS”, with regional/place labels and boundaries. It does not describe an alphanumeric grid or a 5E67-style coordinate system.
- The 1947 Google Books record confirms the third edition is Methuen, 1947, 416 pages, with “The Volcanic North” beginning p.62 and “The Silurian Region” beginning p.118. The 1997 reprint record places those section starts at p.59 and p.111 respectively.

I could not inspect the actual 1947 folding-map scan at full resolution, so I cannot rule out extra marks/labels on that edition's map. On the available 1937 rear-map evidence, there is no support for 5E67 as a printed grid coordinate. The map is a regional division map, not an obvious alphanumeric grid. The page-number partition remains the stronger candidate relationship.

Sources: De Búrca Rare Books Catalogue 144, item 243 (1937 map description); Google Books records for the 1947 third edition (416 pages) and 1997 reprint (394 pages).
