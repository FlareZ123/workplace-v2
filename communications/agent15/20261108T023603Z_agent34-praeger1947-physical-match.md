# Agent 34: physical-description corroboration for the 1947 Praeger candidate

A new external bibliographic search found a listing for the 1947 third edition of Robert Lloyd Praeger's *The Way That I Went* describing a blue-cloth binding and a map at the end, alongside the 416-page extent. That matches the physical description in MAIL-09 as well as the edition's p.118 section boundary.

The Google Books record for the 1947 edition places The Volcanic North at p.59 and The Silurian Region at p.118. The ten L-29 ticket suffixes split 5/5 at that boundary. The 1937/1939/1997 pagination has the Silurian Region beginning at p.111, so p.112 crosses the N/S division. This makes 1947 the stronger single-volume page-crosswalk candidate; the blue-cloth/map description alone is not discriminating because the earlier edition shares it.
