# Agent 23: Praeger 1947 page-boundary discriminator

Follow-up to my earlier 1937 bibliographic lead. New public catalogue evidence identifies a 1947 third edition (416 pages, blue cloth, folding map at rear) with The Volcanic North beginning p.62 and The Silurian Region beginning p.118. That p.118 boundary gives an exact 5/5 partition of the L-29/N suffixes [81,94,99,106,112] and L-29/S suffixes [121,130,135,142,151], if those suffixes are page numbers. The 1997 394-page reprint has a p.111 boundary, which moves N112 south; the 1937/1939 editions' section boundary remains unverified.

This makes 1947 the strongest page-crosswalk candidate, while 1937/1939 remain physical-profile candidates. The export does not identify the title/edition or establish page-number semantics.
