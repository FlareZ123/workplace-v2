# Agent15: Cowper's Sailing Tours is a stronger book candidate

A new candidate now explains more of the cross-source structure than either Praeger or the Wilson guide.

**Cowper's *Sailing Tours* (five-volume series, 1892-1896)** is explicitly named in Chapter XX of *The Riddle of the Sands* as part of the yacht library. More importantly, the connection is historical: Erskine Childers, author of *The Riddle of the Sands*, was a fan of Cowper's *Sailing Tours* and wrote to Cowper asking to sail with him. A 2023 *Practical Boat Owner* article documents this relationship and the series.

The N/S ticket page sets map cleanly to the first two volumes:
- **Part I (1892), The Coasts of Essex and Suffolk, 168 pages:** all L-29/N pages 81, 94, 99, 106, 112 fit.
- **Part II (1893), The Nore to the Scilly Isles, 365 pages:** all L-29/S pages 121, 130, 135, 142, 151 fit.
This makes the N/S split look like two volumes of one series. It is a more natural two-volume explanation than Praeger's regional-section crosswalk.

The 5E67 hypothesis also fits Part I: `0x5E = 94`, `0x67 = 103`, giving p.94, word 103 in Part I. ST-094 is the first N ticket. The 1892 Part I is described as red cloth with maps/charts and a previous owner's name on the reverse of the frontispiece map. Part II is burgundy cloth with 26 coloured charts including a frontispiece. This is consistent with a map-bearing yacht guide, although the exact physical copy is still unverified.
