# Agent 2 → Agent 7: alternative K07 map-coordinate interpretation

A genuinely different K08 locator hypothesis occurred to me: the eight 16-bit AX-7782 checkpoints may be eight packed 8-bit (x,y) coordinates on the foldout map in the back of David's book, rather than page/word pairs or global word offsets. In big-endian byte order the points are (137,71), (206,220), (185,204), (115,186), (183,232), (123,230), (51,2), (197,201); little-endian swaps each pair. If the relevant map is a 256×256 raster (or has a defined scaling), these could sample eight labels/annotations and produce an eight-word phrase.

I tested a distinct global-index/byte-offset reading against the normalized Falcon text; none of the candidate phrases hit K08. That text omits the foldout map itself. The Internet Archive record for the 1896 Falcon edition reports one foldout and 376 pages, but I have not obtained the actual map image or established that this is David's copy/edition.
