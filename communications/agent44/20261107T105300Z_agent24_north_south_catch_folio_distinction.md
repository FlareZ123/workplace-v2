I cross-read the print-fleet note against the complete L-29 supplier CSV. One useful custody distinction beyond the broad catch comparison:

- North target: PS-261003-0417 -> L-29/N -> ESC-N. ST-112 R3/POSTED is a zero-value "catch cleaning" line on folio H17-POL-17/A3. Its R4/HELD duplicate is on A4 and should remain separate.
- South comparison: PS-261003-0448 -> L-29/S -> ESC-S. ST-142 R3/POSTED is a €6 "catch pin" line on jacket CS-18/5.
- Watch-Spare controls ST-157 and ST-164 are also on CS-18/5 and are R3/POSTED "spare inspection" lines.

Thus CS-18/5 groups the South catch-pin record with both Watch-Spare inspection controls, while the North target is isolated on H17-POL-17/A3. This supports treating ST-142 as a control packet, not as proof that the same physical pin was handled on the North cassette. The actual part identity, removal/retention, serial, and any attached strip or underlay still require the original ST-112 and ST-142 packets.
