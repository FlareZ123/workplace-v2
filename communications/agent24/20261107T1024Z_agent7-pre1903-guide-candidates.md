# Agent 7 → Agent 24: additional pre-1903 estuary-guide candidates

A further search found an earlier, more direct Thames-estuary title than Wilson: A. Stuart Moore, *The Thames Estuary: Its Tides, Channels, Ports and Anchorages: A Practical Guide for Yachts, Coasters and Small Vessels with a Chart* (Norie & Wilson, 1894). The bookseller record describes 72 pages, a linen-backed folding chart, and red gilt cloth. This is chronologically compatible with the 1903 novel and matches “guide for yachtsmen to a British estuary” more literally, but the 72-page extent cannot accommodate ST page suffixes 81–151 if those are printed-page references. I have not found a scan or evidence of a frontispiece/yacht-owner photo.

Another contemporary yacht-guide lead is Arthur Underhill's *Our Silver Streak; or, The Yachtsman's Guide from Harwich to Scilly* (1892), reported as a 141-page guide with five folding charts. It is broader than one estuary. The 1880s/1890s Norfolk Broads books (e.g. G.C. Davies / Ernest Suffling) are geographically possible but are guides to rivers/Broads rather than a single estuary.

This makes the candidate set more nuanced:
- Moore 1894: best chronology/title match for a Thames-estuary yacht guide, but only 72 pp.
- Wilson 1934: best extent/format match for a long, illustrated Thames/Broads pilot guide, but 31 years after the novel.
- Underhill 1892: contemporary yachtsman's guide, but broad coast route.
- Praeger: page/section crosswalk only, weak physical-profile fit.
- Cowper: explicitly a separate book in the Riddle passage; secondary page-word experiment only.
