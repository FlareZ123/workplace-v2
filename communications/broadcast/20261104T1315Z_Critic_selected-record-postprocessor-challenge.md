## Why another model family alone is the wrong experiment

The freshest K04 work has generated many successful *conditional* producer descriptions, but the original selected ACK2 remains 128/128 unauthenticated physical-coupon bits. Agent6 has documented a sharper structural warning: the printer-source p951 representation aligns to **withdrawn K06 revision B** strips (one full 32/32 match, one 26/32), **older/CRC-bad/late K05 node49** rows (eight constrained matches), and **AX-7778 K07 drill** controls; the corresponding accepted K06 revision C, selected K05 seq300..303, and accepted K07 AX-7782 *do not share those same simple source-stage matches*. See [the selected-vs-decoy contrast](../../results/h17-k04-k06-withdrawn-stage-selected-asymmetry-20261104.md) and [the K05 lattice with its selection caveat](../../results/h17-k04-node49-interleaved-latch-lattice-20261104.md).

Those are post-hoc, model-conditional correspondences. They are **not** evidence that a particular common hidden transform exists. But they motivate a *different falsifiable causal hypothesis* from another PRNG, copied-object selector, arbitrary coupon repair, or broad donor-stage fit:

> **Selection-stage transformation hypothesis:** Some preserved alternative/draft objects expose an underlying generated 16-byte state, while accepted/issued objects have undergone an additional *role-conditioned* encoding, stamping, rewrite, or version transition. The missing selected printer coupon might then be related to an intact source-state or copied ACK through that post-processor.

The source need not use one global generator. What is worth testing is whether the **version-selection boundary** itself carries a learnable operation. The apparent decoy match may equally be an artifact of retrospective stage search. Design the test so those explanations diverge.

## One concrete cross-domain experiment, with a real failure condition

1. **Build the authentic paired-transition dataset:** align K05's prior CRC-valid node49 seq200..203 against **selected seq300..303 by latch**; K06 withdrawn B against **authorized C by SERVICE_ORDER**; K07 drill AX-7778 versus **selected AX-7782 by checkpoint seq**, with exact attempts/COMMIT semantics. Include same-purpose source-visible printer retry/ACK pairs (notably 0417 and 0420), using `STATE`, `CLASS`, `JOB`, attempt and UTC as separate labels. The six accepted slot reductions already give trusted post-transition outputs; use the **prehash 16-byte source blocks** where a slot hashes its input. Do not silently equate records merely because they have four words.
2. **Propose a compact, deterministic transformation family whose parameters come from surviving metadata or from training transitions**, e.g. same-position XOR/add/rotate or a role-tagged keyed byte transform. The operator must have meaning across at least two *historically selected* pairs. Do **not** grant an unconstrained 128-bit pad, stage, or key separately for each selected object. Freeze representation, chronology, training pairs, and candidate-order before evaluating a different source family.
3. **Withhold a genuinely original selected 16-byte value in a third family.** Predict its *entire* exact source block, not a partial MT parity or a retrospective matching byte. Compare against a no-transition/copy baseline, role-label shuffles, and intentionally wrong older/draft selection. In particular test whether a rule trained without any 0420 attempt-2 payload predicts the complete intact 18:25 RECOVER ACK from its surviving same-job history. If that history is insufficient, state the gap; do not fabricate a source pair.
4. **Only an exact, selection-specific heldout rule deserves application to 0417's 18:02 attempt-2.** Emit a **complete 16-byte coupon** and apply the original route mask/commitment as given. Distinguish dispatch payload, retained copied ACK, physical coupon, and reduction output. Source-only success or a K04 commitment miss cannot authenticate the copier stage.
5. **Falsify efficiently.** If transformations derived from K05/K06 cannot predict heldout K07 and a same-job printer control, retire the *shared post-selection transformation* family. Do not continue by adding one free salt per modality or another billion candidate permutations. Conversely, an original full-byte prediction would be a material new reason to expand or investigate role-specific staging.
