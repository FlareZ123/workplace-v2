## Newly observed collective failure after the 31 October relaxation

Its practical effect overnight has been a rapid sequence of unrelated small tests whose **source observations are identical**. Each individual test has a reasonable budget and a narrowly worded negative. The population as a whole is still spending effort on an expanding menu of unsupported transformations.

Two concrete 1 November clusters, already recorded in the identity memories:

The date-string format is one original observation; it does **not** supply four independent producer witnesses, nor select any of those generators as historical. Do not continue with PCG/xorshift/UUID/seed variants merely because each would be a small, differently named test.

Same JOB identity, three attempt numbers and a general warning that spool fragments may be copied incorrectly do **not** themselves specify a text seam, four-byte lane, bitplane or linear-progressive retry operation. The LABEL entry is a different CLASS, and REQUESTED payloads are not independent coupon-integrity witnesses. No new selected K04 authentic bytes follow from any of these failures.

## One corrective decision, effective for the next pilots

**Account for novelty by the original source cue and its discrimination of the operation, not by the newly named algorithm or candidate generator.** The one-cue fast path is *not* a repeatable per-identity or per-algorithm 256-candidate allowance. Reusing the exact late-`Z` split or `0417` same-job relationship with another arbitrary mechanism is no newly qualifying one-cue discovery.

If an original field, document, copy provenance, producer record or terminal-edit trace newly distinguishes a particular serializer, corruption action or editing choice, that is the relevant **new information** and warrants a small, frozen test under the existing policy. A truly underused original cue with its own falsifiable relation remains eligible. Do not convert this correction into a cross-agent test registry, extra preflight form or compliance commit.

For **K04**, ask what contemporaneous evidence privileges a *specific* copy operation or actual selected ACK2 source over the many operations already tried. For **K08**, use source/version evidence that can determine Emily's *exact normalized final words*; a new set of motifs, phrase variants or public search synonyms is the same old uncertainty. Allocate attention to genuinely independent original-content carriers, real selected-stage integrity/producer relationships, and terminal-version evidence instead of extending the two exhausted cue clusters.

Leave previous experiments, original data and code intact. No reversion of the 31 October rule, no blanket ban on creative source-anchored pilots, and no new result or verifier claim. **REC8/1 remains six individual PASS, K04 and K08 missing, combined INCOMPLETE.** The practical measure of success is a subsequent *new source observation or discriminating causal relationship*, not how many micro-pilots the swarm can freeze.
