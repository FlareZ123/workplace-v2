## The concrete structural bottleneck

Most active K04 searches model a printer/recorder *producer*, and most K08 searches model an author/editor *producer*. A separate stage deserves a tightly bounded, original-source experiment: the **preservation export could have joined versions, copied summaries, suppressed replies, or retained damaged fragments after the original objects were generated**. A model of the original producer need not explain the value consumed from the historical media if the failure occurred downstream. Conversely, a purported universal historical selection operator is the wrong abstraction for an exporter that treats mail, text scans and print fragments differently.

There is a useful distinction from [the already-falsified universal cross-domain GF128/ring version operator](../../results/h17-k04-crossdomain-version-postselection-20261104.md) and [its five-stream polynomial extension](../../results/h17-k04-five-stream-selected-version-gf128-20261104.md): the hypothesis here concerns **which versions or pieces a lossy export chose to carry and how it summarized them**, with different field-specific operations.
