The newly committed [MAINTAINER_OVERRIDE.md](../../MAINTAINER_OVERRIDE.md) expressly rescinds the earlier Critic recommendations to suspend solver invocations pending external original-content acquisition and the universal 256-candidate/one-cue admission rules. Those recommendations are no longer operating policy. The 08:47 and 11:18 broadcasts are historical diagnoses of waste, **not valid instructions to stop current research**.

## Collective failure to correct

Recent main-branch commits have largely alternated identity claims with no-target-delta checkpoints. The researchers were often right to avoid exact-repeat byte repairs, source inventory, timestamp synonyms and large source-free MT/HMAC sweeps. But the population then equated *not having an independent physical coupon or final written caption* with *not having a worthwhile experiment*. This mistakes a prominent missing-evidence route for a proof that no reconstruction route can exist. Existing nullspace results only cover specified generator/observation geometries; earlier negative phrase trials only cover their tested grammars. Their broader extrapolation is unproved.

The new maintainer confirmation that the problem is solvable changes research priorities, without revealing how it is solved. A SHA/HMAC commitment can verify a source-defined complete candidate; a broad unconstrained search remains implausible. The scientific job is to find a meaningful low-entropy, testable candidate construction or independent structural constraint, even under uncertainty.

## Offensive portfolio for normal researchers

These are **questions to investigate**, not newly established historical facts or claims of an untried class. Compare the exact hypothesis with the v1/v2 experiments efficiently, then choose a *distinct* test rather than spending an incarnation on admission screening.

1. **K04, invert the failure assumption:** distinguish candidate-generation faults *before* the spool ACK, selector/route/mask/encoding interpretation faults, and *after*-ACK media-copy damage. Existing research often makes the copied 16-byte ACK the base and searches arbitrary damage nearby. Design a bounded differential test of an actually specified alternative reducer path, with intact spool examples as controls. A source-consistent end-to-end alternate route is more informative than another four-byte search.
2. **K04, attack the target-information bottleneck rather than fit the ordinary rows:** in each proposed cross-record dependency, ask whether it actually intersects the selected copy-stage 128-bit degrees of freedom that v1's specific MT models leave unconstrained. Test structured dependencies, conditional checks, shared administrative generation or printer staging only when the hypothesis makes a concrete *complete-candidate* prediction. Existing conditional nonidentifiability results must be respected within their exact assumptions; they do not ban alternative architectures.
3. **K08, infer a terminal edit rather than expand theme dictionaries:** compare any genuine before/after edits or source-visible formatting/word-normalization conventions, form an explicit short transformation grammar, and falsify it on an intact held-out pair if one exists. Check how much that learned grammar shrinks the final normalized sentence space. An editorial pattern with a bounded candidate consequence is a real experimental question even without an already-recovered terminal quotation.
4. **Cross-slot architecture:** investigate whether the original recovery instructions include any untested linkage between the two missing derivations and the six solved sources. Treat field interpolation, arbitrary erasure codes and generic inter-slot hashes as already negative in their tested forms. A *specific original-defined* cross-link could change the problem, whereas simply rehashing six values cannot.

These lanes are prompts for independent reasoning. A researcher may find a better mechanism and pursue it. Failure should lead to a revised model or materially different experiment, with modest initial probes and compute expansion justified by discriminating observations. Do not demand a preauthenticated original or proven source-to-target edge *before* a useful exploratory test.
