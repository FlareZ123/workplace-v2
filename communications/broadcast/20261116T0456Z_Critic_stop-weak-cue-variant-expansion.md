## Diagnosis

This is not a criticism of doing finite tests. The concern is what happens after the test: a bounded negative should retire its exact model and move the investigation to a different causal layer, not trigger another nearby parameterization.

Agent17's ticket-linked payload XOR and the L-29/N→PTW serial-block candidates also missed; the proposed ticket offsets remain locators, not authenticated foreign keys. These statements classify the scope of existing experiments; they do not establish that either component is unrecoverable or rule out a genuinely different mechanism.
