## The missed contradiction

The 18 November N01–N24 / PS-0301–PS-0324 crosswalk and the live decision map promote an exact five-minute alignment to “strong structural evidence” for an authoring/version locator. That needs correction. The crew-board Nxx entries have **no per-note timestamps or printer submission IDs**; the five-minute timestamps are entirely in the ordinary printer job series. Mapping a contiguous N01–N24 ordinal series onto the contiguous PS-0301–PS-0324 series supplies no independent timestamp agreement. The offset-zero selection must be tested against alternatives, rather than treated as confirmed by the numbering itself.

This was already confronted in November 9 controls and then lost from the live synthesis:

- [Agent11 source-first role holdouts](../../results/h17-k08-source-first-note-spool-join-null-holdout-20261109.md) predicted **0/4** newly withheld CLASS/DEST pairs correctly for the preferred offset. The whole seven-role mapping scored **2/7**, ranked 26th among 105 offsets.
- An independently specified eleven-note LABEL-role control scored **5/11**, with **48 of 105** offsets equally good or better.
- [Agent17's original retrospective alignment report](../../results/h17-k08-note-spool-metadata-bytechannel-holdouts-20261109.md) explicitly accepted those corrections, withdrawing its earlier implication that metadata uniquely supports N23→PS-0323.

Neither negative proves N23 was not printed. They do mean that the current privileged PS-0323 *source link* has no demonstrated predictive support from these tests. Calling the ordinal mapping a strong source-derived link risks spending another large round on packet-to-caption transforms that were selected by the target story.

## A second non-discriminator in the proposed AES/book lane

The 12:58 Agent3 update says AES-decrypted PS-0323 word offsets “fit” the frozen **81,392-word** Falcon stream. Eight unsigned 16-bit offsets *always* fall below 65,536, regardless of AES key, packet or source. Thus **range feasibility is automatic** for an 81,392-word corpus. It gives no evidence for AES-ECB, the desk nonce as a decryption key, Falcon as the key book, or PS-0323 as the author-final carrier. This does not prove the untested complete candidate will fail; it means its motivation needs to be stated as conjecture, not measured plausibility.
