A generator that can interpolate all training points and predict the missing selected record only because the selected record is unknown is not yet a source-predictive mechanism.

## A different next research unit: blind reconstruction of an intact source

1. **K08 editorial process:** For an author-specific grammar, run a *masked-known-caption experiment*: withhold one genuine Emily text (N02 or N06, keeping its label/genre and other available context but excluding its literal wording), freeze the proposed edit/selection procedure, and ask whether it recovers the normalized known sentence at useful rank. Repeat with another holdout if the model purports to generalize. N02 and N06 are described as drafts, and N13 is narrative; do not assume these constitute independently authenticated before/after pairs of the *final* margin. Failure on this benchmark does not logically rule out a unique final edit, but it should stop extrapolating the same uncalibrated template into another million HMAC guesses. A stronger direction is to find actual wording/version/custody cues that constrain *literal* syntax, not just sea/night/table topics.

2. **K04 event-state bridge:** Treat REQUESTED, ACK, transmitted/copied buffer and physical coupon as different stages unless a source demonstrates equivalence. Build a *pseudo-selected* reconstruction of one intact, withheld RECOVER/ACK record from prior-stage original observations using the same proposed reducer. Check the complete 16 bytes before using the K04 verifier. Existing Agent13 work reports no intact same-job, same-class native-versus-copied selected-stage pair; if no suitable stage control exists, state exactly which assumption is untestable and seek a different stage observable. Do not treat source-fitted GF interpolation or synthetic recurrences as a historical printer model merely because they produce complete selected candidates.

3. **Allocate the next substantial effort to finding discriminators, not maximizing candidate counts.** A useful positive is an independent original packet or phrase recovered before its bytes are shown, an authenticated source transformation, or a cross-record rule that survives such a holdout and predicts the selected event. If the blind test fails, change the causal model. A small, falsifiable mechanism with a clear failure diagnosis is better than another unconstrained family of algorithm names, digest wrappers or thematic synonyms.

This is an **experimental recommendation**, not a compulsory pre-authorization gate. The maintainer permits uncertain and large attacks where genuinely justified.
