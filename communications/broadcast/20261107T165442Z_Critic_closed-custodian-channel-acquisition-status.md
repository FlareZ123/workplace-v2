## Correction to the 10:20 wording

My 10:20 broadcast described the 7 November addendum as a “coordinated source request.” That wording was ambiguous and should not be read as permission or a recommendation to recontact the maintainer/custodian.

The current root README records the 21 October final reply: no further human records or archival referrals will be supplied, and the closed custodian channel must not be reopened. The 10:20 wording did not intend to override that boundary. I am correcting it explicitly.

## Status of the acquisition documents

Keep [the custodian acquisition addendum](../../results/h17-custodian-acquisition-addendum-20261107.md) and [the missing-evidence request](../../results/h17-missing-evidence-request.md) as **conditional preservation and intake specifications**. They are useful if an original becomes available through an independently authorized existing source, an already-preserved local image, or unsolicited delivery. They specify what to preserve, which controls matter, and what provenance is needed.

They do not establish that a requested object survives, that anyone will provide it, or that a new access route exists. Do not send, relay, repackage, or repeatedly expand requests to the closed custodian channel. Do not treat a longer inventory of hypothetical workstation caches, folders or records as source acquisition.

A new acquisition lead should name the actual authorized access route and the concrete locator or independent evidence that makes the source reachable. If that is not yet known, label the lead conditional and preserve it once. Do not create a workflow per artifact or endpoint.

## Research remains open

This is not a research hold and does not imply that the export is unsolvable. The maintainer's solvability statement remains in force. Continue with genuinely new source-grounded mechanisms, existing local evidence, and independently accessible original sources. Keep speculative source leads separate from acquired records.

For a retrieval claim, report inspectable original content, record/page identity, raw bytes, hashes and provenance. For a reconstruction claim, report the complete candidate and the unchanged component commitment result; keep historical selection/authenticity separate. A locator, workflow success, request list or metadata-only response is not recovery.

Preserve all existing experimental evidence and the six accepted components. K04 and K08 remain unresolved until the original acceptance checks pass.
