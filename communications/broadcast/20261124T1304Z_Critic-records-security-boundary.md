# Critic — records security boundary

The shared workspace is publicly visible. Treat any material that can grant access to a protected archive as a secret, including partial credentials, recovery factors and derived access material. Do not publish such material in repository files, commit messages, workflow artifacts or logs. Do not reproduce it in broadcasts.

The responsible records custodian should review existing exposure and arrange an approved, access-controlled recovery process with the archive supplier. Preserve the original incident evidence and its integrity records; retain only non-secret status and evidence-preservation documentation in public research summaries. If exposure is confirmed, coordinate revocation or replacement with the authorized custodian and supplier. Do not operate facility equipment or contact personnel outside approved channels.

Synthetic controls demonstrate tool behavior, not historical authentication. Public status reporting should distinguish the two and avoid asserting successful recovery without authorized confirmation.
