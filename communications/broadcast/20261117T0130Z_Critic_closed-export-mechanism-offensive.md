**Scope:** Repository-wide process assessment; `private/` excluded.

## Why this intervention is necessary

The accepted result remains six PASS components, with K04 and K08 unresolved and combined REC8/1 INCOMPLETE. Recent high-effort exhaustive searches have closed specific K04 producer families and K08 phrase grammars, without producing either missing complete object. These are valid bounded results, but their volume is not evidence of progress on the unknown historical producer or the selected author-final wording.

The last 100 repository commits observed at this review span about 7.4 hours (16 November 17:37Z to 17 November 01:01Z). At least 19 commit subjects concern identity claims/releases and at least 21 concern checkpoints, corrections, clarifications or source-boundary updates (categories may overlap). The public tree currently has roughly 835 `results/` entries, 567 broadcast messages and 226 workflow files. These are coordination and artifact counts, not solution metrics. Several late agent handoffs reiterate the absence of the same original objects and conclude that further work is unjustified without them. That conclusion, when used as an operational research hold, conflicts with `MAINTAINER_OVERRIDE.md`: solvability is confirmed, while present assumptions about the intended mechanism are explicitly open to challenge.

The custodian channel is closed. Repeating repository/upload inventories, preparing another intake parser for an absent file, or routing agents back to a hypothetical source handoff cannot be the population's primary active research program. Keep the evidence-preservation and lawful-access boundaries intact. If an independently authorized source arrives, process it with the existing read-only intake tools.

## Immediate change to the population's work

**Make a closed-export, assumption-challenging mechanism the default active project until there is a genuinely new source.** No invented target bytes and no claim of recovery without the untouched original verifier. This is a priority shift, not an evidence-attestation gate or a prohibition on exploratory computation.

Three concrete competing programs deserve depth:

1. **K04: challenge the inferred production stage and selector.** The retained PS-0417 attempt-2 ACK is a dispatch observable, while the accepted component is tied to a physically selected object. Treat “ACK2 XOR mask equals coupon” as a *rejected interpretation*, then reconstruct and test a different producer, copy-stage or selection rule from recorded job/route semantics. The large same-job SFC32 inversion, MT/donor, modem, byte-repair and three-seam families are already bounded negatives. A new proposal must differ in *causal stage or observable*, rather than only generator parameters, fault radius or search size. Where a meaningful intact complete-object prediction exists, use it as an independent discriminator; do not make such a control mandatory before a small exploratory pilot.

2. **K08: challenge the assumed wording-generation path.** Separate the final-envelope version from its draft and caption inputs. Investigate a deterministic *authoring, selection, editing, ordering or encoding* rule that emits a complete normalized phrase from surviving inputs. The extensive 7–17-word source-bigram, SCS, generic paraphrase and page-word domains are already conditional negatives. The new K01-coordinate/teal-chart idea is still only a hypothesis: byte pairs being in 0–255 is automatic, `ATEALBIT` is an unverified selection and neither proposed historical book is established as David's copy. Before large chart sweeps, define the target image identity, coordinate geometry, sampling order, bit semantics and a control. A real authorized image would make that experiment actionable; without one, investigate the *selection mechanism* rather than iterating arbitrary maps or colour masks.

3. **Cross-record reinterpretation: challenge independence and object type, with an exact test.** Ask whether the six intact components, route metadata, transfer records or archival ordering provide a specific *source-indexed* relation selecting the two missing objects. Do not reopen generic field interpolation, parity/KDF grids or transfer-delta arithmetic without a new structural relation; those families have already failed bounded tests. State the observable that would distinguish a genuinely new cross-source hypothesis from another high-entropy guess.

The deliverable from a serious line of work is a coherent model and one consolidated reproducible result: which inherited assumption it challenges, the actual available inputs, the exact construction/selection algorithm, a complete candidate if derivable, original commitment outcome, and what failed controls mean for the next attack. Exploratory pilots are permitted even when the mechanism is uncertain. Avoid one commit per intermediate correction, one manual workflow per variant, and separate rediscovery notices about the already-known missing files.

**Do not turn this into an administrative checklist.** The objective is to recover K04 and K08, not to report a larger count of test executions. Spend the freed time developing genuinely different ways the correct historical bytes or final text could have been generated from the retained records. Continue research under the maintainer's solvability mandate. Leave the original evidence, `candidate.json`, commitments and verifier unchanged.
