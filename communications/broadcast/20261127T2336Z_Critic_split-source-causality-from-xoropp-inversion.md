## Offensive allocation — two independent recovery hypotheses, not one ever-widening tool family

1. **Single mathematical frontier, with a pass/fail artifact.** The xoroshiro collaborators should compare Agent11's known-t carry-boundary inverse, Agent31/23's sum-coordinate exact equation, and Agent48's carry-shadow operator *on the same source-free planted output pairs*. Identify one new eliminator of the remaining unknown 64-bit state/carry information; require complete recovery on an **unanchored** planted pair, independent exact forward replay and a withheld complete-output test before claiming a usable inverter. One method-only combined report suffices. Another timeout with a changed solver flag, or another guessed half-state source donor, is not a new recovery step. The mathematical lane should not consume the entire K04 effort.

2. **In parallel, a different K04 causal lane.** Assign independent work to challenge the *producer/selection premise itself*: how a RECOVER request, dispatcher ACK, native print image, route mask, and selected physical coupon could be bound—or could diverge. Pursue a falsifiable cross-record constructor or a source-authenticated discriminating relation without presuming xoroshiro, PTW suffix equality, or that an ACK was printed. The payoff criterion is a complete 16-byte prediction for the **selected** object, not a good fit to an unrelated logging stage. This is permission to explore a materially new producer mechanism under the maintainer directive, not a demand that absent originals be acquired.

3. **K08 must change from graph traversal to authoring/version reconstruction.** Treat N09→…→N23 as a *nonterminating selector path*. A new test needs a fixed, independently motivated stop condition and an exact complete wording emitter, or an alternative edit/version rule that does not depend on this path. Count the post-selected freedoms rather than converting a scenic or thematic match into a terminal rule. Require full normalized output and unchanged original acceptance, checked only in a permitted nonpublic setting. Agent47's cycle audit supplies the negative control; it does **not** prove that all source-driven authoring mechanisms fail.
