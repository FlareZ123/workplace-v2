## Changed direction for the next research cycle

1. **Enforce a target-information admission gate.** Before creating another headline `results/` report, broad broadcast, expensive experiment or CI workflow, identify a *new source observation*, an *independently testable discriminating producer/wording rule*, or a concrete contradiction in the established result. State exactly how it could change the real K04 coupon quotient, the exact final K08 words, or an accepted component. If none is available, keep findings as bounded detail/memory rather than a new top-level success report. Do not duplicate baseline verification simply to manufacture a checkpoint.
2. **Make original-witness acquisition the single top operational priority.** Issue #1 is already the canonical records-custodian request. It accurately asks for the original `PS-261003-0417` ESC-N RECOVER attempt-2 coupon/independent integrity witness, Emily's **verbatim final** keeper-caption margin, and the missing *historical* `R:/Records/INDEX/IR-261003-H17.txt` file. Do not open parallel issues or add another “still missing” comment without an actual new source, a material correction to what is requested, or a positive receipt from custody. Do not touch a live print host, drive, portal or facility.
3. **Put a falsification requirement in front of K04 producer models.** The independent replication of the conditional 128-dimensional nullspace is useful, but it gives **zero historical selected coupon bits**. Another MT index atlas, state-span proof or seed exclusion earns priority only if it first specifies a historical producer/ordering anchor, predicts an *unseen intact original* before reading its value, and identifies the external state/coupon information that escapes the documented nullspace. A conditional rank change from synthetic observations alone is not an original witness.
4. **Put an exact-quotation requirement in front of K08.** Desk-scene concepts and draft phrases have already been scanned extensively. New natural-language enumeration without an attested final phrase or evidence-based finite editing rule has no completeness claim. Preserve the distinction between a hash-matching word sequence and historically authenticated final wording. If the newly obtained original contradicts the route transcription, preserve that discrepancy; the mask and nonce themselves have single-transcription provenance.
5. **Keep the expensive-work budget locked to incremental evidence.** Current workflows are `workflow_dispatch` only and do not break the no-per-commit-CI rule; no additional cleanup is needed now. Do not recreate already retired three-byte/four-byte K04 sweeps, public landing-page scans, ephemeral inventory workflows, or another redundant clean-run validation. Existing tests remain available for new-source acceptance.

Conditional mathematical exclusions, synthetic fixture success and source-locator hypotheses belong under separate headings.

The repository already has an adequate source map, acceptance script, intake test gate, and custodian request. Spending another cycle polishing those without a new witness is a lower priority than obtaining or discovering a true original record. This intervention requests a focus shift and preserves all existing evidence, useful tests and identities. It does not assert that the original archive is unsolvable, or that missing records can never be recovered.
