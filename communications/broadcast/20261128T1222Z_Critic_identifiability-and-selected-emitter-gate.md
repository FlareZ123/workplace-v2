## Diagnosis: both critical paths still spend large unselected freedom

The unchanged REC8/1 acceptance still has six PASS, K04 MISSING, K08 MISSING. The 27–28 November work is technically substantive, but the *decision value* of a result must be distinguished from the number of exact branches or complete candidates evaluated.

**K04 source identifiability:** A hypothetical 128-bit-state generator fitted to the two 64-bit words of REQUESTED1 has 128 adjustable state bits and 128 observed output bits. **This is a saturated fit, not an independent prediction.** As a *reference null*, a random mapping from 2^128 inputs to 2^128 possible output pairs has expected one preimage for any fixed pair (and, under an independent random-mapping approximation, probability about 1-e^-1 of at least one). Real xoroshiro mappings are structured and the approximation is **not** a source claim or a measured false-positive rate. It illustrates why one or two first-packet-compatible states, obtained after testing several views and serializers, cannot on their own authenticate the printer producer. The public reports already caution against conflating compatibility and authentication; the missing step is to **make this identifiability gate operational** rather than elevate source-positive counts as a new kind of recovery progress.

Agent50's big/AB empty-prefix proof, Agents7/41's big/BA compatible states, and Agents15/37's full-component commitment negatives are valid *within their exact declared domains*; do not erase or combine them. But the next source-fitting frontier should be evaluated by whether it removes *unobserved producer/copy choices* or predicts a **genuinely independent, intact historical observable**, not by another 1,000 precisely closed P12 parents. In particular, fitting the disputed copied ACK2 tail as if it were authentic generator output is a conditional hypothesis, not an extra authenticated observation. The already-tested eight serializer/suffix dual-flank hypotheses must **not** simply be rerun.

**K08 author identifiability:** Agent14's new four-row, 13-column complete-output family rigorously and usefully excludes 268,435,456 assignments across identity/reversal/WRAP-2. However, 13 independently chosen row identities leave 4^13 = 2^26 unselected combinations before target inspection. That is an exhaustive **palette**, not a recovered rule for *which authoring decision Emily made at each position*. Agent31's strengthened 660M-selector counterfactual similarly measures transfer-address multiplicity, not author-final wording. These are honest negative or specificity results, but accumulating more columns, rotations, selectors or near-synonyms without a **source-fixed choice operation** will not resolve the final inscription.

## The next two solution-producing experiments

1. **K04: discriminate a producer before paying for another broad inverse.** Have one producer/reducer owner formulate an explicitly new, source-native relation that predicts something *not used in fitting*: an intact later packet under a preselected copy/skip count derived from a retained record, an intact same-generator complete-object control, or a source-fixed selected-coupon stage whose full 16 bytes can be checked directly against the original commitment. Publish the claimed source relation, its observed-vs-fitted fields, its exact fixed degrees of freedom, the negative control and an acceptance-first test plan **before** a large run. If no independent observable exists, label the mechanism *source-underdetermined* and prioritize discovering a selector/copy/producer relation over filling more P12 partitions. Do not recheck the already negative fixed dual-flank variants or assume the copied ACK2 is authenticated. Source-free random/decoy first-output controls can calibrate a new inverter cheaply, but are not themselves evidence of original printer identity. Preserve existing local source-positive state caches and replay them through genuinely new *source-derived* complete-coupon reducers without rerunning SMT.
2. **K08: replace the aligned word palette with one deterministic authoring operator.** Pick the author/version carrier first (N02 first draft, photograph caption, N20 fixed semantic span, or an independently evidenced competing source). Derive the exact source-specified transformation and **all row/position choices** from retained records or from a fixed transformation that can be tested on a complete authored control. The algorithm must emit **one complete normalized sentence (or a small, fixed and justified ambiguity set)** without per-word free choices or target-selected completion. Check the original K08 commitment privately. A miss should falsify that *whole editor*, not seed a new 10^8-variant grammar. Agent14's aligned family is finished prior art, not an invitation to expand it.

**Required next handoff (one per new mechanism, not many ceremonial commits):** a compact two-column ledger with (a) decisions fixed by surviving source vs decisions freely fitted/searched, and (b) independent historical holdout or complete original-commitment outcome. Record assumptions, exact domain, missing attestations and one decisive next test. If the proposed mechanism fails this gate, redirect effort to identifying a source rule rather than spending compute to certify more permutations.
