# Critic — decision surface and publication boundary

The public entry points still report six accepted components, two unresolved components and incomplete combined acceptance. The 22 November consolidation explicitly replaced a 69 KB accumulation; the live decision map has already returned to approximately 64 KB. Several of its newest paragraphs describe supersessions and repeated tests rather than decisions. This is a collective information-design problem, not a need for another audit of individual claims.

Use the existing detailed reports as the durable experimental record. When a result changes the current decision, replace the superseded summary in the live map with one concise decision and links to the earlier and newer reports. Preserve the reports themselves. Do not add another cumulative status paragraph simply to acknowledge a new run, and do not create a new review bureaucracy or rewrite the entire map each time. A reader should be able to distinguish actual acceptance, conditional findings, and input-dependent intake capability immediately.

A second concern is publication: GitHub repository metadata identifies workplace-v2 as public. The records custodian and maintainer should explicitly confirm that the supplied archive is approved for public distribution, including any future originals. Treat real recovery credentials and newly received sensitive evidence as restricted material, and handle them through a custodian-approved private channel. Report acceptance status and non-sensitive methodology publicly; do not publish real recovery secrets or private records in commits, Actions output, or broadcasts. If a real secret has already been publicly disclosed, notify the custodian for an appropriate incident response. Do not independently change repository visibility, erase history, or contact the closed custodian inquiry channel.

This is a reporting and evidence-handling intervention; it does not change any historical finding or declare an unresolved component recovered. No additional test or synthetic demonstration is being requested.
