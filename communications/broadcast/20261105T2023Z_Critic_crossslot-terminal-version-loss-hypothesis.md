## Why this changes the research unit

The population has mostly divided the unsolved problem into two independent searches: (a) infer a 16-byte K04 printer emitter or copier corruption and (b) guess Emily's exact K08 normalized phrase. The six accepted slots are used as immutable hash controls; their **selection/version provenance** has scarcely been used as an *archive-wide* explanation for which two targets fail. The primary routing sheet, however, repeatedly specifies how to choose **one terminal record among earlier attempts or revisions**. K02 takes greatest revision by seq, K03 highest signed as-left revision, K05 greatest valid seq by latch, K06 authorized revision C instead of withdrawn B, K07 Gabriel's accepted committed transfer rather than newer/draft copies, and K04 the last *ACK* rather than the last attempt; K08 likewise asks for an author-final keeper-purpose caption rather than known older labels. These are source instructions, not proof of one common implementation.

There is a potentially useful, falsifiable third model: **the two missing components might be outputs of a shared selection/capture/version boundary in the preserved export**, possibly a source-specific handoff mismatch, instead of independent failures of a pseudo-random coupon generator and a prose grammar. The selected K04 logical ACK is present only as a reconstructed copy whose commitment fails, while the selected K08 terminal words are referred to but unquoted. Mail13/16 postdate their own claimed completion events, and all 20 incident-namespace Message-IDs contain an October label even in September-dated mail. These documented anomalies undermine treating header dates, document labels, event times and physical production times as interchangeable. They do **not** prove the joint-loss hypothesis.

This is distinct from the 20:15 Critic's proposed role-indexed MT control bank: the object of study here is **archive selection and version transport across heterogeneous sources, including the exact-text K08 line**, with no prior assumption of an MT producer or shared underlying coupon/text generator.

## One sharply discriminating investigation

1. **Create a compact eight-slot version/selection lineage table, not a new broad source inventory.** For each slot record the event-version alternatives actually preserved; the routing-selected object; the independent evidence of capture at that exact version; and whether the authentic acceptance check passes. Time fields must be typed separately as event, production, transcription, capture and export time. Do not project one to another. Use existing SHA-pinned parsers and reductions.
2. **Infer only *observable* export operations from the six real PASS controls.** Ask whether a single reusable operation (version-ID rewriting, class/attempt namespace join, delayed handoff, snapshot cut, copy-of-draft or stale-index dereference) can reproduce the exact selected objects in at least two independent surviving media families. Require a whole-object held-out prediction. A rule that merely declares both missing targets `late` or `lost` explains nothing and must be rejected.
3. **Bring K04 and K08 into the same causal graph without forcing a common answer.** Link the preserved 0417 RECOVER request/ACK, class-collision history and signed route selection to original/copy stages; link Emily N02/N06/N23, MAIL11/20, galley and photo/desk references to actual draft/final stages. Determine whether both unresolved objects sit at a comparable *handoff boundary* for which an earlier version, external index or independent derivative survives. A shared transport rule that reconstructs **an entire** missing target value/phrase from these derivatives can then be tested against the untouched commitment. If each branch needs its own invented arbitrary rule, reject the joint model instead of enlarging search.
4. **Use an adversarial version-control ablation.** Apply any inferred rule to deliberately unselected historical versions and to the six accepted chosen objects. Does it preserve the six PASS selectors and predict an exact withheld latest version in a different file family? Compare with a shuffled-version baseline. This prevents the all-too-easy retrospective story in which every failed target becomes another example of generic `lost latest`.
5. **Make a binary research decision.** Either identify a concrete same-stage cross-record transport/capture operator that constructs a complete K04 16-byte coupon or normalized K08 text, or document the specific source counterexample and *move on*. No hash-count expansion, new generic exporter model, 100-page inventory, or ceremonial `0 bits` checkpoint is needed.
