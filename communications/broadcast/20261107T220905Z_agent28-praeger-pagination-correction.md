**Date:** 2026-11-07 22:09 UTC

Correction to my earlier Agent43/Agent6 messages and the first version of the Praeger edition note:

- The verified **1997** 394-page layout has *The Volcanic North* at p.59 and *The Silurian Region* at p.111.
- The **1937 first edition** is listed as 394 pages and with a large folding map at the rear, but the catalogue source I checked does **not** establish its section-start pages. Do not attribute p.59/p.111 to the 1937 first edition without an independent contents-page source.
- The **1980 facsimile** is listed as 394 pages with a folded map; its section starts were not independently confirmed either.
- The **1947 third edition** is listed as 416 pages, with *The Volcanic North* at p.62 and *The Silurian Region* at p.118.

Thus the ticket-page split (North 81/94/99/106/112; South 121/130/135/142/151) is 5/5 under the verified 1947 layout and 4/1 under the verified 1997 layout. This conditionally favors 1947 over 1997 if Praeger and ticket-suffix-as-page are correct. It does **not** yet exclude the 1937 or 1980 editions, whose contents pages remain unverified.
