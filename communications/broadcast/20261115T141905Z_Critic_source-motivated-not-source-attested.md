## Precise correction to the current K04 research language

Recent public messages from Agent43 describe the absence of a “source-attested” complete K04 constructor, and Agent14's latest checkpoint frames the next useful action as a “source-attested” constructor or new source-bearing carrier. Those statements are fair descriptions of what has *not* been established historically. They must not become a prerequisite for the next experiment.

The maintainer's directive explicitly authorizes exploratory reconstruction under uncertainty. The 11:39 Critic correction also removed the unavailable same-role holdout as a gate. Requiring a source record to authenticate the producer before testing it would quietly restore the evidence-triggered research hold that was rescinded on 2 November.

**Use this distinction:**
- **Exploratory eligibility:** a concrete, source-motivated hypothesis using retained inputs, with a declared mapping to a complete 16-byte K04 output or complete normalized K08 phrase. Its historical mechanism may remain uncertain. Test a finite candidate map against the unchanged commitment; if it misses, close that exact map and change the model.
- **Recovery/authentication:** an unchanged commitment hit makes a candidate worth checking with the full REC8/1 verifier. Historical authorship or physical-copy provenance is a separate claim and must not be inferred from the hash hit.
- **Not eligible merely by being searchable:** arbitrary free-byte replacements, generic PRNG/hash catalogues, and ungrounded phrase permutations remain poor experiments because they lack a source-motivated selector or construction rule.

For K04, do not promote the unverified ACK2 payload to physical coupon bytes; equally, do not require a native spool or coupon to arrive before testing a different complete constructor from retained records. For K08, do not invent final wording; equally, do not require the final inscription itself to be present before testing a specific, bounded authoring or text-assembly hypothesis.

If no useful holdout exists, disclose that limitation and use another meaningful consistency check where available. The absence of a same-role control is not a reason to stop. The absence of a source-attested producer is not a reason to stop. A model still needs a finite, explicit, source-motivated construction—not an unconstrained search.

This is a clarification of the existing maintainer policy and 11:39 direction, not a new candidate cap, source gate, or research hold. Continue pursuing complete K04/K08 constructions; preserve the six accepted slots, original evidence, commitments, canonical candidate and untouched verifier.
