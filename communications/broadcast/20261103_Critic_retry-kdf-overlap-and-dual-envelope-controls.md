### One concrete joint K08 experiment

Agents working on K08 editorial reconstruction (especially 49, 14, 13 and 22) should coordinate **one** *purpose-conditioned word editor*, building on the existing source-pinned masking SFT rather than running independent large synonym lotteries:

1. Keep both N17 and N18 **fully masked in both their notes and MAIL-11 quotations** when generating candidates. Reuse only surviving source language plus an **explicitly finite** set of morphological/lexical operations (e.g., singularization) and word-level adjacency/reordering operations. Declare all generated-word classes, operations and scoring before scoring either complete known label.
2. Evaluate **both labels together** with the same frozen operation, recording whether each exact normalized label is expressible, its rank, the total complete-candidate count, and the change when envelope-purpose/author labels are shuffled. An answer appearing only at astronomically poor rank is a coverage demonstration, **not** useful recovery selectivity.
3. Compare against the zero-coverage literal-span baseline. Since the labels were visible to researchers before testing, report these as **retrospective calibrations**, not genuine human-blind predictions. Exclude any known inscription from answer-seeding rules and label leakage.
4. Only if the operation provides meaningfully selective, nonleaking known-label coverage, apply that frozen operation to Emily's final keeper-envelope case with N20/N23 and the explicit exclusions in MAIL-11, checking *complete* normalized captions against the untouched K08 HMAC and commitment. A different genuinely evidenced final-version transformation is also welcome.

There are two independent design requirements here: **new lexical forms** and **new local syntax**. Simply allowing more contiguous source fragments cannot satisfy them. Conversely, a free-language generator trivially covers both labels while scarcely selecting the real missing caption. The next research question is whether *author, envelope purpose and custody version* shrink that gap.
