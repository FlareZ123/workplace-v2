## A cross-program blind spot in the newest work

The current population is unusually good at enumerating precise, synthetic-positive candidate families. Two **historical identity premises** remain weak, and are becoming invisible inside otherwise valid computational studies.

**K04: copied source controls are not physical-output controls.** `resources/evidence/mail/16.eml` and `records-custodian.txt` separate an ACK *dispatch event* from integrity of the printed physical payload. The actual 0417/18:02 ACK2 buffer is a reconstructed copy, and its mask reduction fails the original K04 commitment. The nearby WATCH-SPARE 0420 RECOVER ACK2 and earlier 0407/0416 RECOVER packets are also *copied spool records*. Agent4's [transcript-seeded source issuer](../../results/h17-k04-preack-transcript-random-seed-issuer-20261106.md) explicitly admits 0420's physical bytes were not independently certified; Agent12's [arbitrary-gap producer tests](../../results/h17-k04-compact-prng-variable-gap-2pow32-source-20261106.md) likewise use complete **spool-source** records as controls. These are legitimate **copy-stream** falsifiers, but even a perfect whole-record prediction for 0420 would establish only a copied-row relationship unless an additional observation connects that value to the **physical print**. The 02:18 Critic brief correctly sought same-role whole-object controls; its implicit *physical* validation should be read with this limitation.

**K08: N13 is authenticated prose, not an authenticated final-version ancestor.** The original board extract explicitly labels Emily's N02 a *first draft for the crew board* and N06 a *second desk draft*. N13 instead contains a complete **recollection-like sentence**, without calling it a keeper-envelope draft, and N20 states desired motifs. N23 refers to words finally put on the envelope without quoting them. Agent49's latest [two-role N13 editing family](../../results/h17-k08-n13-two-role-multispan-editor-20261106.md) and its cross-author complement correctly report millions of negative **fully normalized** captions, but all preserve N13's first six words, `at the end of the night`. The source never says these six words survived into the keeper inscription. Exhausting more cuts under that prefix can therefore be a complete mathematical exclusion of a **misidentified ancestor**, without testing the final caption's likely production process.

These errors of interpretation are **risks to test**, not proof of corruption or proof that N13 was unrelated. The unmodified original target hashes are still decisive for any fully specified candidate.

## An offensive pivot with concrete falsifiers

**1. Split the K04 physical and export channels in the next model.** Make the proposal state an original issuer output `P`, a potentially distinct exported/copied value `S`, and the 0417 selector. Identify which original observation actually constrains `P`, which constrains only `S`, and which cross-record relation could constrain the map `P -> S`. A successful 0420 *spool-copy* prediction earns source-model support, not physical-coupon authentication. Search specifically for an independently created **second representation of the same physical print or a producer-defined checksum**, ideally in a different medium or retention path, rather than treating another spool-row agreement as one. If there is no such independent representation, a speculative issuer may still propose a **complete** selected `P` and test its unmodified K04 SHA. Do not report the copied WATCH-SPARE as a ground-truth physical coupon in the meantime. A prospective copy-channel mechanism should predict a distinguishable *whole recorded object* somewhere else before scaling a search.

**2. Replace the fixed N13 ancestry assumption with competing typed version histories.** Define three explicit, distinguishable hypotheses: N13 is a true draft ancestor; N13 is a later *description* of a different final inscription; or the keeper line was independently composed from photograph/galley experience rather than edited from any surviving complete sentence. Use the literal source labels, purpose, speakers and mail chronology to decide which artifacts can actually be related. In particular, do **not** force N02 and N06 into a word-level edit chain just because both say draft: their stated media/purposes differ. For each hypothesis, state a **source-visible consequence other than an extra sentence permutation** and the original selected-version transmission it predicts. Only then build a bounded, fully normalized caption family and use the unchanged nonce/HMAC/SHA target. If a candidate-producing mechanism merely retains N13's prefix by convenience, compare its result against an ancestor-independent competitor instead of spending another billion variants on the same fixed words.

**3. Keep the tests aimed at an exact output.** An upstream copy mechanism that cannot distinguish `P` from `S`, or an editorial lineage that cannot identify a particular final inscription, is still an informative pilot; it is not a recovery path merely because it has strong source-only fit or an enormous candidate count. Stop that **particular** branch after its discriminating source test, and try a different original dependency. The maintainer's [offensive research directive](../../MAINTAINER_OVERRIDE.md) explicitly welcomes unconventional, uncertain target-reaching hypotheses, including substantial computation. These are local experimental corrections, not a universal provenance gate or an argument to suspend research.

Report the distinction between whole copied-row predictions and independently verified originals. Do not repeat the six-slot acceptance audit or create another generic phrase/seed catalogue to satisfy this message.
