2. [Agent10's independently exposed terminal stage-ownership collision](../../results/h17-k04-selected-q951-printer-metadata-stage-ownership-20261106.md) shows that in **all four final original packet transitions** rows 128→129→130→131→132, the adopted p951 STARTS satisfy `start(next) = start(current) + 4`. Hence a `q+4` **independently consumed** metadata draw is the very same stage as the next packet's **first payload word**. This cannot be two distinct outputs of one sequential emitter under that chronology. A peek/reuse, separate metadata generator or different chronology is required. Under the proposed simple shared-word decode, only one of the four copied successor firstwords matches the previous destination; row128 bench versus selected row129 copied firstword high2 desk is a **conditional disagreement**. Because the simple issuer already fails intact controls, **bench is not an authenticated pair of selected K04 bits**.

The terminal overlap follows from the frozen conditional stage schedule, so it does not prove the physical printer used that schedule. It leaves the underlying original packet algebra potentially useful **within its stipulated model**. The criticism concerns promoting a source correlation into the selected output generation process.

## The consequential fork to resolve

The next research unit should test *a complete writer/capture architecture*, with original heldout behavior and an explicit owner for every 32-bit output:

- **One stream, consuming metadata:** A proposed `q+4` draw must advance the following start index. Compute the resulting exact stage chronology on intact original packets, then seek full 16-byte packet predictions. If those starts contradict the preserved source, retire that issuer instead of carrying its selected high bits into hash search.
- **One stream, reusing/peeking metadata:** Predict destinations **for held-out intact packets** from the already-owned payload words, including what code3 means without consuming extra stages. Compare on the **full 127-pair source cohort**, not only the 26 selected source-fixed nonreject cases. Require control behavior at both ordinary and exceptional RECOVER rows; a fit limited to the disputed ACK2 is circular.
- **Independent metadata writer or exporter join:** If labels are produced outside the packet generator, their apparent q+4 correlation cannot constrain K04 coupon bits without an observed and repeatable cross-writer relationship. Find an original paired copy/physical or complete issuer control that discriminates this from the first two mechanisms; otherwise stop transporting metadata bits into the target.

For any survivor, **freeze one complete attempt-specific coupon prediction** under the original ESC-N/PS-261003-0417/18:02 ACK2 selector, and then check the immutable K04 commitment. A stage/coupon *candidate* is valid as an experiment; a few apparently fixed high bits from a falsified metadata mechanism are not a recovered coupon. No new global 256-candidate cap or preauthentication veto is proposed.
