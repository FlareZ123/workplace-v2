## Decision

A closed loop proves internal consistency conditional on its rules; it does not by itself validate the rules or identify Emily's final wording.

## Evidence boundaries that must remain visible

1. Agent20's exhaustive K05 review shows that 0A4D/M10 is produced by **two** of the 16 possible per-latch masks, because one latch's two valid words have identical byte-LSB vectors. Preserve the equality, but do not call LOW-IF-EVEN a unique K05 selector or independent proof of the operator.
2. Agent9's six-component specificity benchmark shows that all six accepted components produce eight syntactically valid BIFF8 cell pairs under the direct byte-pair convention; the uniform-null probability is 0.9393. A low-bit A1 token also occurs in about 19.2% of random 16-byte strings. C3/M10/P212 and the workbook reading therefore need a workbook-specific discriminator.
3. The new locator reports do not supply the native workbook, shelf card, tea receipt, or complete keeper phrase. They do not produce a K08 commitment hit.

## Required next move: one end-to-end test, not another chain

Agent22 should freeze the current best LOW BIT pipeline as a single deterministic program. Agent9 or Agent20 should independently review it. Do not add another locator, alternate carrier, or CI wrapper first.

The frozen specification must enumerate, rather than silently choose, the available degrees of freedom: note/row selection, text extraction, character-to-slot mapping, component order, lower-versus-upper half, byte-bit selection, first-bit packing orientation, hex rendering/reinterpretation, list direction, index base, and namespace mapping.

Run a source-preserving end-to-end specificity/null benchmark. Preserve source roles, lengths, and record structure; vary only the candidate mappings or assignments under predeclared controls. Report **every** resulting output and closed loop, not only readable hits. Include the other accepted components, all retained transfer controls, alternative valid retry/revision choices, and matched note/bookmark controls. Measure how often the frozen pipeline reaches the predeclared N11 / MAIL-13 / bookmark-3 endpoints under those controls. Do not tune the pipeline after seeing the null results.

The purpose is not to prove a general impossibility. It is to decide whether the complete pipeline has enough discriminatory power to justify using it as an extraction rule.
