This edit does not cancel already running jobs or prohibit a new bounded brute-force hypothesis with a concrete reason. It prevents additional unconditioned 2^40 per-offset dispatches through this reusable entry point.

**K04 source-to-coupon mission:** Investigate a producer *at the selection/copy/serialization boundary*, rather than tuning another underdetermined MT state. A tractable starting discriminator is whether routine same-job, multi-class or multi-attempt records support a coherent, forward-predictive request-to-ACK or LABEL-to-RECOVER transformation. The direct fixed-XOR/additive transitions and neighboring swaps are already negative. Test a materially different emitter on intact nonselected rows with a declared blind whole-packet prediction. Only after it demonstrates source-specific predictive content should it be used to emit an entire selected PS-0417 attempt-2 coupon and check the unchanged K04 commitment. Conversely, a source observation that makes the target's 128 free bits identifiable would also be a decisive new contribution. Re-examine the selected job semantics when justified by the source, including the distinction between dispatch ACK and physical coupon.

**K08 text-to-final-version mission:** Prioritize one executable complete-phrase rule from the retained author/editorial chain (N23 margin, MAIL-11/MAIL-20, dated desk notes, photograph-caption links). Test the rule on earlier exact author-visible phrases or a held-out version when available, produce a *full normalized candidate* if it is genuinely determined, and run the official HMAC/commitment check. STOW14, LB10 and M10 are conditional locators; more metadata-format positives without historical carriers do not create words. Specifically challenge whether the author-mask/codebook and the N23 note-job association conceal a recoverable whole-text edit or selection operation instead of extending already tested lexical grammars. Treat any new decoding scheme as exploratory unless independent source features distinguish it from postselection. Alternative hypotheses are welcome.
