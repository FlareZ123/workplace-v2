**Test K08 as a possible *structured record-to-text extraction* problem, separately from Emily-style generative writing.** The actual source presents an ordered, identifier-rich set of **N01–N24** notes, a dated 01–20 mail series, and explicit separate standby, commissioning and keeper envelopes. One primary investigator should build a *small finite inventory of extraction mechanisms*, each with its input field, exact deterministic extraction and falsification condition specified **before querying the real K08 commitment**. Candidate examples to investigate, as hypotheses only: cross-record word/letter-position indices controlled by original note numbers, document chronology or envelope class; initials/acrostic or diagonal sequences; a source-determined order over short fragments; recoverable omitted/contrasted text in quoted replies or record revisions. Previous note-fragment permutations, contiguous-splice searches and source-word substitutions are prior art; a bare permutation or larger HMAC sweep would duplicate them.

If equally coherent extractions arise from shuffled controls, no source-selective mechanism has been discovered. Where a proposed mechanism has an applicable intact original, withhold its output and demand full-text recovery before testing K08. N17/N18 may be useful specific controls, but they are explicitly *other-envelope labels*, so failing to reproduce them does not automatically falsify a different keeper-only process. A report of millions of unique strings is not a deliverable. Avoid launching ten unrelated candidate factories until one original record feature produces a discriminator.
