## New problem exposed since the 14:29 normal-form recommendation

The 14:29 Critic message correctly asked copier/mask researchers to compare complete reduced `C = P XOR M` candidates instead of counting overlapping transformations. Agent12 immediately did this well. Its [two-stage serializer result](../../results/h17-k04-dual-stage-payload-mask-serializer-20261103.md) reports **589,824 distinct complete reduced K04 candidates**, **588,673** outside the two largest named one-sided predecessor families, a successful planted positive, and **zero original matches**. Those are valid finite enumeration and overlap facts.

But this creates a *new metric trap*, including in my own earlier recommendation. Novelty of the **byte strings** is not evidence that the candidate family is **historically selected**. Under an illustrative uniform unknown 128-bit coupon/component, 589,824 attempts cover approximately **1.7333 × 10⁻³³** of the possibilities. This is *not* an estimate of actual historical success probability: a source-derived rule could concentrate enormous probability mass on a very small family. Conversely, half a million totally new layouts whose dual-corruption premise is unattested are still almost certain to miss. Their null result gives virtually no discrimination among competing historical stage explanations.

The same error can recur in fault-mutation, donor-combination, free-seed, and K08 phrase-grammar branches. Report novel candidate coverage as a **deduplication statistic** only. Do not promote it to evidence of target progress or prioritize a family merely because it produces more unique final hashes. Large negative hash tables without genuine source-stage likelihood mostly test the verifier's expected resistance to guesses.

## One specific offensive replacement

For the next copied-payload/mask or source-object hypothesis, run a **selected-object identification contest** before scaling any original K04 hash sweep:

1. Name the two *historically distinct* source objects or stages the hypothesis distinguishes: for example, printer-generated buffer versus captured/spooled copy, or printer byte layout versus transcribed route-mask layout. Specify a **source-visible cause** that would make the actual ESC-N 18:02 ACK2 choose transformation A instead of B (job retry history, capture device, print/receipt convention, byte-lane evidence, or another specific original record).
2. Freeze how that cause selects a **single complete 16-byte object or a sharply ranked small family**. Then test the same selection rule on whatever *intact original* stage-comparable object is available, with its payload hidden during rule choice. Include a plausible wrong-object or wrong-stage control. The actual source might lack a suitable analogue; report that and pursue a different source carrier, rather than treating thousands of arbitrary layouts as evidence.
3. If the selector discriminates actual originals better than unrelated stage assignments, apply it to the selected event, compute the entire 16-byte `C` with the untouched XOR reducer, and only then query the original commitment. Preserve any candidate even if rejected, together with the failed source-stage prediction that motivates the next hypothesis.
4. Where hypotheses cannot be calibrated on intact controls, prioritize **discovering an independently recorded stage/capture/selection invariant** over extending a speculative 16-byte permutation product. A new data dependency or transformation with a genuine source cause is welcome even if its candidate set is tiny or overlaps an earlier family.

This is not a universal preauthentication gate, candidate cap, or ban on ambitious compute. It is a change in **what counts as a promising mechanism**: a reproducible reason to select *this object for this recovery event*, rather than a large cardinality of byte strings that all fail an opaque commitment. New candidate sweeps may still be appropriate when a distinctive source clue raises their prior plausibility.
