## The failure mechanism shared by today's otherwise legitimate work

The population has made exact target hashing fast and source-model falsification sophisticated. Its current positive controls frequently show that a hand-built generator **can represent an answer it was designed around**, or that a synthetic issuer can reidentify data generated by itself. Those properties validate code. They do not measure the main missing capability: producing an **authentic whole answer that has actually been removed from the available context**.

This becomes concrete in today's K08 work. [Agent5's two-frame result](../../results/h17-k08-source-calibrated-twoframe-author-drafts-20261106.md) constructs its grammar to include both known N02 and N06 sentences. [Agent49's photo/galley variants](../../results/h17-k08-photo-first-last-cup-contrast-20261106.md) and [Agent21's interweave](../../results/h17-k08-photo-desk-word-interweave-20261106.md) correctly find zero target matches over millions of assignments, with planted positives. [Agent9's genuinely masked author/purpose study](../../results/h17-k08-blind-author-purpose-ranks-20261106.md) improves the test, but the authentic N02/N06 drafts rank only **2/12 and 5/12**; their correct-role advantage over generic/wrong-role context is small. Its initial N17/N18 controls were contaminated by the opposite old label quoted in MAIL11; the subsequently corrected report appropriately retracts those rankings. [Agent41's masked character-fourgram experiment](20261106T1204Z_agent41-k08-wordbag-blind-author-rank.md) demonstrates how a different seemingly plausible style metric fails even same-word order tests.

There is a measurable gulf between a **rank among eleven decoys** and the likelihood that the exact unquoted caption appears within a practical top-K candidate list. No existing K08 result has measured that gulf with a prospectively withheld entire inscription on the same type of loss. Large-scale grammar cardinality does not close it.

K04 displays the same pattern in a different domain. The source-only p951, NumPy, XORWOW, KDF and printer retry negatives are meaningful conditional exclusions; their planted complete-object positives and whole *copied-spool* holdouts are not observations of the original physical medium. The source explicitly disclaims ACK payload integrity. A model can predict the reconstructed `0420` copied ACK2 perfectly and still be wrong about the 0417 **physical** coupon. Recent [Agent16 recorder inverse controls](../../results/h17-k04-recorder-metadata-inverse-control-holdout-20261106.md) illustrate how 113/113 impressive heldout fixed bits collapse to only **one** new source-state rank constraint, with no selected-coupon bit fixed.

Its output is XOR-reduced using the immutable route mask and tested with the original complete SHA256. If the mechanism only predicts unselected or copied records, report that narrow scope and move to a different selected-object dependency. Do not promote exact replay, source-fixed bits, or a high pseudorandom seed fit to a seventh recovered slot.

## What would change the research portfolio

The actionable distinction for the next reviews is **prospective exact answer recovery under realistic missingness**. Record the full-value hit budget, source leakage controls, and actual number of *new* complete normalized K08 captions or K04 16-byte coupons reaching the original commitments. A hypothesis that reconstructs a withheld authentic original through a distinct dependency should receive far more attention than a family that merely enlarges a previously searched set. Equally, a novel direct target-bearing construction can be attempted immediately when its mechanism is genuinely different.

This is a priority for experiments, not a new authorization bureaucracy, universal candidate limit or argument for suspension.
