## Direction: resume constructive exploration without turning controls into admission gates

The maintainer authorizes meaningful exploratory tests before historical provenance is fully established. Apply that authorization in practice:

1. **Each missing slot needs a genuinely different complete-candidate construction attempt.** K04 work should leave the repeated p950/p951/p952, CPython/HST call-order, donor-permutation, mask and small byte-fault families alone unless a new observable changes the model. K08 work should leave generic literary scans, semantic paraphrase sweeps and the already-tested N02→N06 operator alone unless a new source relation changes the construction.
2. **Do not require an eligible historical holdout when the supplied export contains none.** Where a suitable complete control exists, predict it before fitting. Where none exists—as Agent 4's K08 graph currently reports—state that limitation and proceed with a source-motivated, bounded complete-candidate model whose assumptions are explicit. Test complete candidates against the unchanged original commitment. A commitment hit would establish a candidate, not by itself historical authorship or provenance.
3. **Do not make an original coupon, final inscription, authenticated selector, or new external record a prerequisite for every exploratory attack.** Those remain valuable evidence targets when an authorized route exists. They are not a substitute for testing different interpretations of the records already supplied.
4. **Prefer one owner per concrete model and adversarial review after the model is frozen.** Do not parallelize an undefined idea or send repeated requests for someone else to invent it. A useful negative should change the next attack; it need not spawn another broad broadcast.
5. **Measure progress by complete target-bearing output.** Prioritize a complete K04 16-byte candidate, a complete normalized K08 phrase, a new source-derived constraint on either target, or a materially new construction mechanism. Partial rank, locator strings, synthetic positives, successful workflow execution and another list of unavailable records are not recovery.

## Reduce administrative fragmentation

- Bundle a coherent investigation's code, result, relevant communication and identity-memory update into one checkpoint/commit where practical. Stop making a separate commit for every one-file note or handoff.
- Do not publish a result for every local negative. Preserve durable reproducibility when it will help a successor; keep scratch observations in the working investigation until they form a meaningful result.
- Keep `results/README.md` decision-focused and update it only when the best recovery decision materially changes.
- Do not add a new one-off workflow for a nearby parameter variant. Reuse existing scripts and workflows where appropriate. There are no active or queued workflow runs to justify a CI cleanup campaign, so preserve dormant reproducible workflows rather than deleting them ceremonially.

This is **not** a research hold, candidate cap, requirement for a source-first holdout, or claim that any particular solution mechanism is known. It is a correction to the population's tendency to turn uncertainty into repeated process and provenance work. Keep the original evidence, candidate, commitments, route and verifier untouched.
