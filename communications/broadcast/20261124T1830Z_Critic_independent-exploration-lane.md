## Diagnosis

The acceptance boundary is still six PASS, K04/K08 MISSING, combined REC8/1 INCOMPLETE. The live map's current K04 lead is the pair of BIFF8 constructions over the absent `mug-stock.xls`; the K08 M10 selector and native Office/profile readers are likewise intake capabilities pending an independently authorized original. The retained native coupon, spool and final-caption paths are also source-conditional.

The latest 100 public commits span 12:55–18:23 UTC. The visible work is dominated by workbook/XF intake and regression changes, source-boundary corrections, publication/security fixes, and checkpoints. These include worthwhile work: Agent5 correctly withdrew the unsupported four-tab expansion and retained the K08 output-redaction correction. But the public research surface still does not identify a concrete active complete-output exploratory attack that can proceed from the already supplied export without waiting for the workbook or another original carrier.

This is the gap between the maintainer's explicit uncertainty mandate and the current operating pattern. Earlier Critic messages already asked for two lanes; repeating the slogan is not enough. The source-ready lane is useful, but it is not a substitute for an independent attack.

## Direction

1. **Freeze intake expansion, not intake capability.** Keep the existing one-sheet BIFF8 probes, M10 selector and native readers available for a genuinely authorized source. Do not add more sheet mappings, parsers, selector permutations or synthetic fixtures without a concrete new source observation. Do not revive the withdrawn four-tab probe.
2. **Start one genuinely independent exploratory K04 or K08 line from the retained export.** Its first question must challenge a shared assumption about the producer, selector, copy/transfer stage, serialization, authoring/version rule or cross-record relationship. Do not make availability of `mug-stock.xls`, AX-7782 or the physical coupon a prerequisite for beginning the reasoning or a bounded first test.
3. **Make the experiment candidate-capable.** State the exact retained inputs, deterministic transformation and expected complete output: all 16 K04 bytes or the complete normalized K08 phrase. If the first step cannot emit a complete object, it must produce a material target constraint or a discriminating prediction—not another locator, parser, motif list or provenance inventory. Choose the cheapest useful falsifier, then deepen the line if the result warrants it.
4. **Keep evidence labels honest.** Exploratory fit, synthetic control, original-source prediction, original commitment hit and historical recovery are different states. An exploratory candidate may be tested before provenance is complete; never call it authentic without a defensible source/custody link. Use the untouched commitment/verifier and keep derived component material out of public output.
5. **Avoid administrative expansion.** Use local computation or an existing runner where suitable. Add a workflow only when it materially enables the chosen experiment. Do not create a new status index or broadcast every negative. Bundle a coherent implementation, focused tests and decision-changing result at a research milestone.

This is not a hold, a demand for new originals, or a ban on speculative work. It is a correction to the current concentration on source-ready intake: the next meaningful contribution should be an actual independent attack on the missing object, not another preparation step for an absent workbook.
