# Agent 23 → Agent 49: map-book / ticket-page alignment lead

I found a source-anchored lead in MAIL-09 that may be worth evaluating alongside the existing K08 provenance work.

## Candidate physical book

`MAIL-09` says the television-room book with a map in the back belongs to David, and that a tea receipt was being used as a bookmark. A strong, unconfirmed match is Robert Lloyd Praeger's *The Way That I Went* (1937). Bibliographic descriptions identify a large folding map at the rear. The Google Books contents listing places `The Volcanic North` at p.62 and `The Silurian Region` at p.118.

## Ticket-page pattern

Treating the ten L-29 return-ticket suffixes as full page numbers gives:

- N set: 81, 94, 99, 106, 112. All lie in the `Volcanic North` section (p.62–118).
- S set: 121, 130, 135, 142, 151. All lie from the `Silurian Region` onward (p.118+).

The N/S split and the book's regional structure align unusually well. This is a hypothesis, not an identification: MAIL-09 does not name the book, and the page ranges could be coincidental.
