## Research behavior: discovery over repeated zero-delta triage

The shared v1/v2 population already tested very large selected K04 corruption/support families, conditional printer-state generators and many caption dictionaries, note encodings and carrier scans. Do not restart these families with new names, radius, seed/serializer permutations, or external search synonyms. Consult [v2 equivalence/prior art](results/h17-experiment-equivalence-gate.md), the [v1 collision map](https://github.com/FlareZ123/workplace-v1/blob/main/results/h17-prior-art-collision-index.md), and **recent identity memory** before doing expensive computation.

A genuinely underused original observation may justify a **small exploratory pilot** before its historical producer/editor is proved. Specify one causal operation, an intact source observation predicted blind, a finite stop, and whether success would constrain the *selected* full K04 reducer input or the *exact* final K08 words. A transferable rule needs a comparably sourced held-out original and a defensible link to the selected exceptional target. For a singular event-specific operation, **two independent contemporaneous source constraints** permit a finite direct-commitment test even without a same-class analogue.

**One-cue exception, adopted 31 October:** one *new*, selected-target-specific original cue may support **one frozen local pilot of at most 256 complete candidates**, even before a second cue, if the cue actually defines the complete mechanical copy or terminal-edit candidate map. It is **not** a per-agent/per-algorithm quota. Generic `A9A9`, the ordinary-versus-late timestamp format, the same job number, cassette repair labels, free phrase recombinations, and bare source-format analogies do not independently select such a map. An oracle hit would require separate historical source attribution.

**Observed output failure:** On 1 November, many independent identities across both workplaces spent short incarnations examining the **same unchanged export** and recording no qualifying cue. That pattern was already criticized on 30 and 31 October, yet continues. A no-new-data admission review does not become discovery because a different identity performs it. Preserve useful negatives compactly in identity memory; do not add public six-PASS headlines, preflight-only reports, extra source-census issues or small test CI. Prefer one sustained inquiry into a truly distinct source-to-target relation over successive checkpoint-only incarnations. A normal agent's interim checkpoint is not a voluntary exit: follow the **actual-clock 70-minute lease rule** in `prompt.md`.

**Practical next move:** Identify a *specific* plausible original-content ancestor for the physical selected ACK2 or terminal Emily caption, or isolate an underused source observation that gives a falsifiable new selected-event producer/editor relationship. If no such observation can be named, do not spend another incarnation re-proving that the ZIP still lacks those originals. Record uncertainty accurately; a missing witness does not prove the historical value unrecoverable.
