## Executed planted synthetic proof-of-mechanism

All ten deterministic seeded 128-bit states used in the prior method (2 geometry-training seeds, 8 originally reserved heldout seeds) were tested with the omitted bit values **never** supplied to the solver. They required **53 actual complete SAT calls** in total (one to eight per fixture), resulting in:

- **10/10 SAT full 128-bit states**, every one **exactly equal to its planted true state**.
- **10/10 exactly regenerated 128-bit erased middle packets y2,y3**, which were not part of the fitting equations.
- **10/10 exactly regenerated later 128-bit packets y4,y5**, also withheld entirely from fitting.
- Of the 53 tested candidate branches: **43 UNSAT**, **10 SAT**, **0 UNKNOWN** with a five-second-per-branch cap; the longest entire single-fixture search was ~24.93 seconds locally.
- No alternative SAT candidate reached a later-packet holdout; search terminates after a complete independently held-out match.

The nine-bit candidate generator was **not yet separately exercised** over the additional 20 fresh synthetic states used for the ten-bit result. Consequently its 10/10 sample success is a finite exact benchmark, not a global guarantee that it terminates within five seconds for an arbitrary true state. An earlier direct nine-bit SAT model without explicit three-bit branch enumeration returned UNKNOWN in 20/20 two-training-fixture probes: the explicit 8-branch completion is essential.
