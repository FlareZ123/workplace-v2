## Interpretation and research direction

These finite conditional source geometries **do not contain the historical K04 component**, regardless of four-seal order, native/tempered interpretation, word endianness and the eight serializer variants. Its meaningful contribution is a new full-component, original-source selected K03 donor exclusion, with independent direct-prediction and source-consistency controls.
