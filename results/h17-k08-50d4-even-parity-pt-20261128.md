## Result

The mixed-attempt AX-7782 side-channel selected by the source program **ATTEMPT TAILBIT LOW IF EVEN** emits the two packed bytes:

`50 D4`

The earlier interpretation treated these as ASCII `P` followed by unsigned decimal 212, producing the conditional locator `P212`.

There is a substantially tighter source-local decode:

- `0x50` has even bit parity and its low seven bits are ASCII **P**.
- `0xD4` has even bit parity and its low seven bits are `0x54`, ASCII **T**.
- Therefore `50 D4` is exactly **7-bit ASCII `PT` with an even parity bit**.

This does not require interpreting 212 as a page number, Excel row, or arbitrary decimal locator.

Under the already used A1Z26 note-address namespace, `PT` is **N16/N20**. Those two notes have a unique maximal common run of two words:

**do not**

`... voices at the table. Do not use the standby label just because it is shorter.`

So the transfer side-channel independently emits the same **PT prohibition boundary** used by the recent overlap-chain decoder to stop the reconstructed N20 first-sentence payload.
