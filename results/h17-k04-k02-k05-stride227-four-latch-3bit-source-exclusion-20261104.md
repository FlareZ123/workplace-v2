## Why this is a different attack

The preceding conditional MT printer model from Agent49 fits 132 intact historical printer packets as independent native MT19937 word observations and withholds the disputed selected 18:02 ACK2 completely (source rank **15,365**). Agent1 showed that the actual selected K02 GOOD/HOLD telemetry halfwords, hypothetically observed as a four-word donor at MT stage **1178** or **1405**, reduce the selected K04 native words at stage951..954 from 128 free bits to a **33-bit affine quotient**. Agent34's selected-K05 second-donor investigation considered *contiguous* K05 words at seven longer offsets and individual selected K05 latches at the selected target boundary. Neither covered a **four-latch, noncontiguous writer with an independently unknown draw stride**.

The original K05 target selects four distinct CRC-valid node49/event82 latch records, sequenced 300..303 at 21:14 UTC, 4 raw words rather than one 16-byte independently printed coupon. A producer could, speculatively, have emitted these at four increasing MT positions separated by hidden intervening draws. The **227-word** lag is meaningful in MT19937's 624/397-word recurrence and emerged from the *rank-only* screen without using actual selected K05 values, K04 copied bytes or the K04 commitment.

There is **no original declaration that these sources share a producer or that each 1-Hz latch implies 227 draws**. These are strict conditional candidate models, not physical-record authentication.
