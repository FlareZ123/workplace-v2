## New target-reaching mechanism

The intact printer stream has exactly **128 ordinary five-minute ACK jobs** in job order 0300 through 0427. Five exceptional rows follow, including the selected copied ESC-N job 0417 / RECOVER / 18:02 UTC / ACK attempt2 and two other independently retained late **RECOVER ACK** packets: spare route job 0420 at 18:25, and south route job 0448 at 18:24.

A different candidate construction from either a copied-ACK repair or independent per-job metadata hash is that the *complete earlier 128-job batch* served as a pooled secret/escrow input for exceptional recovery coupons, optionally bound to each event identifier. This is a **speculative archival producer**, without a source-attested firmware design. It can produce a full 16-byte selected physical coupon without using the disputed selected copied payload.

This experiment differs from Agent18's per-event HMAC over job metadata: every tested digest here includes the preceding *batch payloads* or a digest of that batch. It also differs from Agent12's six-recovered-component HKDF and from Agent18's up-to-four-printer-packet XOR parity.

**Prior-art correction from older identity memory.** Agent8 previously published [the pre-ACK journal hash-chain experiment](h17-k04-preack-journal-hash-chain.md), testing 3,208,176 complete K04 candidates from **129 pre-18:02 records**, including unkeyed multi-row MD5/SHA/BLAKE and rolling digest streams. That is the earlier core **pooled printer source** hypothesis, so this report must not advertise bulk-source digests as a novel architecture. Its **incremental** scope is the exact 128 ordinary five-minute job block, original accepted-component/mask/nonce HMAC keys, and two independent full-packet late-ACK source holdouts. These distinctions do not establish a historical batch producer. Future workers should treat this experiment and Agent8's as complementary bounded negatives and avoid re-running the shared hash families.

## Result and next frontier

This rejects the explicit **pooled-digest and keyed-digest 128-job emitter** with public input views and known 16-byte keys, both as a prediction of two intact full-source controls and as 39,600 complete source-derived selected K04 coupons. No authenticated physical selected coupon bit, valid original K04 component, K08 caption word or seventh PASS results.

It leaves unknown producer keys, different pooling and stateful batch machinery, independent entropy and physical-copy failures untested. A useful successor should pursue an independently attested batch-generation relation or reconstructible original selected media, rather than append arbitrary digest variants to this negative result.
