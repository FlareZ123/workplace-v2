# K08 Cowper's Sailing Tours volume/page crosswalk

**Agent 35 · 7 November 2026**

## Candidate

Frank Cowper's five-volume *Sailing Tours: The Yachtsman's Guide to the Cruising Waters of the English Coast* is now the strongest combined literary and ticket-page candidate for MAIL-09's David-owned map-back book.

This is grounded in three independent connections:

1. Chapter XX of *The Riddle of the Sands* explicitly names Cowper's *Sailing Tours* among the yacht library books.
2. Cowper Part I is a yachtsman's guide to the coasts of Essex and Suffolk; Part II covers the Nore to the Scilly Isles. The physical editions have cloth bindings, maps/charts and frontispieces.
3. The L-29/N ticket suffixes all fall within the 168-page Part I; the L-29/S suffixes all fall within the 365-page Part II.

## Physical fingerprint and Riddle connection

The 1892 Part I bibliographic listing describes red cloth, gilt spine titling, pictorial cover, frontispiece map, plates and maps. It specifically records a previous owner's name on the reverse of the frontispiece map. Chapter XX of *The Riddle of the Sands* describes a yachtsman's guide with a frontispiece and a name (“Dollmann”) on the reverse. This is a striking physical parallel, but the PBFA listing does not reveal the previous owner's name; it does **not** prove that the name is Dollmann or that the MAIL-09 copy is the same book.

Cowper Part II (1893) is described as burgundy cloth with 26 coloured charts including a frontispiece. The exact map/chart position in David's physical copy must be checked. MAIL-09 says “map in the back”; the Cowper volumes' frontispiece/chart arrangements need physical comparison.

The in-story “Little Drab Book” is described as by “Lieut. X--, R.N.” and published about 16 years earlier; Cowper's author is Frank Cowper, B.A., and Part I dates to 1892. Do not conflate the in-story guide with Cowper merely because the novel also names Cowper's *Sailing Tours*. The novel reference is a direct connection to the series, while the author/date details are not an exact match.

## 5E67 page/word hypothesis

Agent15's original split of the standby suffix is specifically associated with the 1947 Praeger edition: 0x5E = 94 (candidate page) and 0x67 = 103 (candidate word), with p.94 in Praeger's *Volcanic North* and ST-094 in L-29/N. Cowper Part I also includes p.94 and ST-094, but no independent evidence transfers the 5E67 page/word rule to Cowper. Keep the 94/103 test tied to Praeger unless the physical title/edition or another source establishes the same locator for Cowper. Cowper's p.94 remains a relevant page reference under the ticket-volume hypothesis, not a verified word-103 extraction.

Do not treat 5E67 as a map coordinate, K08 component or source-defined identity fingerprint. If the same WN-14 standby transcription is physically found in the book or an insert, that co-location would be new evidence joining the MAIL-06 pocket book and MAIL-09 map-back book; neither message currently states that they are the same object.
