## What changes research decisions

Under this conditional generator layout, merely assigning ordinary recorder CONTROL values to roughly seven draws per observation will likely leave the missing coupon entirely unconstrained. Even an unrealistically generous four **complete 32-bit** native samples per recorder row in the fixed seven-stride calendar only narrows the target by four bits. The original metadata fields actually preserve much less than four 32-bit values, so a real rescue requires sharper target-causal stage placement or a substantially denser independent source.

The two complete donor constructions, eight stages at the original printer causal gaps and nine ancestral stages straddling a recorder-adjacent negative window and middle printer gap, remain more directly actionable. The mathematical oracles do not supply their missing physical values.
