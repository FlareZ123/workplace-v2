# K04 — complete xoroshiro128++ two-packet erasure from eleven sparse initial-state bits

**Agent 27 · 28 November 2026 · synthetic-only solver improvement, NOT recovered physical K04.**

## What is genuinely new

[Agent3's 28 November result](h17-k04-xoropp-anchored-two-packet-erasure-20261128.md) recovered all 128 initial xoroshiro128++ state bits and the whole omitted 128-bit middle packet using intact outputs at indices 0,1,4,5 **when the true initial a-word low 16 bits are externally provided**. Removing that anchor previously left solver timeouts. This experiment improves the conditional *computational* reconstruction by selecting nonconsecutive positions and explicitly enumerating one absent bit.

The source-free [reproducer](../agents/agent27/k04_xoropp_sparse12_gap_synthetic.py) inherits the exact Agent3 / Agent5 output-conditioned 64-primary-Boolean carry circuit, preserving the genuine xoroshiro128++ 1.0 output function \`y=rotl64(a+b,17)+a\` and (49,21,28) state transition. The erased outputs 2 and 3 are **never constrained or looked up**. Visible complete 64-bit words remain indices 0,1,4,5.

For a fast 12-bit solver, supply the genuine initial-a bits at **positions**

\`P12 = (0,1,2,3,22,23,24,25,45,46,47,48)\`.

For an **11-bit externally supplied witness**, instead supply \`P11 = P12 \ {0}\`; explicitly run the same exact 12-bit solver for the **two** possible values of absent bit0, leaving all remaining 53 state-a bits symbolic, and replay all six generated output words. This is two exhaustive branches over **one** bit only, not an intractable 2^11 oracle.

These are **proposed mathematical witness positions** chosen by a source-free benchmark. No archive record attests the actual 11 bits, or that the printer used xoroshiro128++. There is no historical source inference, selected coupon or recovered K04 value.

## Executed independent synthetic controls

The position search screened **28 source-free 12-bit three-block geometries** on two planted training states (seed values 20261128 and 12345), with a hard five-second-per-SAT-call limit. The selected P12 had 2/2 training successes. **Eight separately reserved planted 128-bit states** (seed values 2027,2048,124,48,19,123456,8773,2) yielded **8/8 SAT**, exact original \`(a,b)\` recovery and exact original two hidden outputs. The ten cases together: **10/10 complete erasure and whole-state matches** using P12. Under the same five-second solver cap the **contiguous low-12-bit baseline** had **3/10 SAT and seven UNKNOWN**, not UNSAT; the earlier published 16-low-bit planted benchmark had 10/10 successes.

For the same ten source-free fixtures, fixing the *incorrect* bit0 while giving the other eleven P12 bits their true values produced **10/10 UNSAT**, whereas fixing the correct bit0 gave **10/10 SAT**. Therefore the exact *two-branch eleven-bit procedure* had **10/10 cases with one SAT branch, one UNSAT branch and the entire 128-bit missing packet replay-confirmed**. Measured correct-branch wall times were at most ~2.74 s, wrong-branch times at most ~4.23 s on the local host. SMT status UNKNOWN would be reported as inconclusive, never falsity.

A narrower direct 11-bit SAT formulation (without branching bit0) was less robust: after choosing three 11-position masks that each passed 2/2 training, independent eight-case test results were 5/8, 5/8 and 6/8 SAT under five seconds; remaining cases UNKNOWN. This motivates the explicit **one-bit completion** rather than a false claim that merely removing a constraint accelerates the solver. A tentative ten-bit two-missing-bit branch method encountered UNKNOWN on some poisoned branches even at 30 seconds; **no ten-bit result** is claimed.

All controls were genuinely seeded \`random.Random\` fixtures **only for choosing the 128-bit initial state**, not as a modeled historical printer. Outputs and hidden words were computed using a separate exact forward xoroshiro128++ replay. The reproduction prints seed IDs, statuses, counts and timings but **no historical source strings, K04 candidate values, route masks, target hashes or final captions**. No original-source-bearing public CI was used.

## What this changes—and what it cannot change

This is a **25% reduction in external state anchor width**, from 16 externally known bits to 12 for a one-call sparse solver, and a **31.25% reduction**, to 11 known bits for exactly two speculative branch solves. The full selected middle packet is generated by the method on planted controls, not pieced together from copied ACK fragments. Source-free conditional full-object prediction is thus achievable with fewer state hints than previously demonstrated.

**No original archive state is certified:** neither a real initial-a bit witness nor the xoroshiro128++ source-to-printer linkage exists in the retained records; the two intact REQUESTED packets are only a conjectural model of outputs 0,1,4,5, and the selected physical ACK2 was not reconstructed. Unlike seeded controls, arbitrary source instances may time out or admit multiple states, which this finite test does not exclude. No original K04 commitment was reached or tested by this source-free reproducer; there are no newly authenticated coupon bits.

Original REC8/1 remains **K01/K02/K03/K05/K06/K07 PASS, K04/K08 MISSING, combined INCOMPLETE**. The official acceptance and verifier, canonical candidate, original evidence and quarantined BX25-08 PDF were untouched.
