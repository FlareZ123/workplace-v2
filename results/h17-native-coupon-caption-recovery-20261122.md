## K04: complete printed coupon, regardless of how it was stored

The original route selects ESC-N / PS-261003-0417 / last ACK; the retained copied 16-byte payload's XOR does not satisfy the original K04 SHA256 commitment, and its physical integrity is not authenticated. This work leaves the route, copy, mask, and commitment unchanged while adding several ways to read **an independently recovered actual coupon** from printer-native media:

- [Raster barcode decoder](../work/h17_k04_image_barcode_intake.py): Pillow with pyzbar/zbar and optional OpenCV reads original QR and linear barcode bytes from PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF, WebP and up to 32 frames of a TIFF. Preserves a separate Latin-1 view when a QR decoder has re-encoded opaque bytes to UTF-8.
- [EMF DIB decoder](../work/h17_k04_emf_dib_intake.py): validates intact EMF/EMFSPOOL page boundaries, reads original `EMR_SETDIBITSTODEVICE` and `EMR_STRETCHDIBITS` bitmap record buffers and passes reconstructed BMPs to the barcode reader; source stream remains unchanged. Microsoft [MS-EMF] defines both bitmap-record layouts.
- [PDF source reader](../work/h17_k04_pdf_barcode_intake.py): gets original selectable PDF text, condensed spaced-hex labels, native annotations and AcroForm values, PDF portfolio/page FileAttachment contents, embedded images, and a bounded 200-dpi rendering for QR/vector coupon graphics. No encrypted PDF is opened without a source-authorized key. PyMuPDF APIs are used as documented.
- [PCL5 raster decoder](../work/h17_k04_pcl_raster_intake.py): reconstructs explicitly sized native monochrome raster jobs from documented unencoded, repeat-byte RLE, or TIFF PackBits rows and recognizes compound escape commands; feeds actual rendered pixels to the same barcode decoder.
- [Preserved mail](../work/h17_k04_binary_window_intake.py): a MIME-aware `.eml` reader scans unmodified attached PDF/image/native printer files recursively with bounded depth/byte budgets, while original Outlook `.msg` compressed-RTF coupon text is decoded by the already-tested [K08 MSG decoder](../work/h17_ax7782_k08_phrase_intake.py). Both carriers are tested under their original complete K04 mask/commitment.\n- [Office authored text](../work/h17_ooxml_text_intake.py): reads source-authored WordprocessingML `w:p/w:t`, PresentationML slide/notes paragraph runs and Excel rich shared-string units from true ZIP/OPC documents; recovers full coupon/author phrase across adjacent styling runs. No rephrasing or office-engine conversion. [OOXML split-run SFT](../work/test_h17_ooxml_text_intake.py) covers source-genuine equivalent document structures.
- [Exact coupon formats](../work/h17_k04_binary_window_intake.py): original raw16 and masked16 bytes, uppercase/lowercase 32hex, grouped bytes, standard padded Base64, plus common printed UUID, Windows GUID, RFC4648 Base32 and unpadded URL-safe Base64 serializations. Printed 32-hex source images also enter through bounded optional Tesseract OCR when native text is unavailable. Every interpretation gets the **original full K04 SHA256 comparison after the original XOR mask where applicable**. There is no fuzzy target acceptance.

The new raster/PDF/PCL/serialization tests share one [single-test-file regression](../work/test_h17_k04_image_barcode_intake.py), including untrusted decoder outputs, corrupted EMF/PCL, encrypted PDF, and direct/masked synthetic coupon positives. The GitHub Actions completed successfully for [PDF](https://github.com/FlareZ123/workplace-v2/actions/runs/45053376797), [PCL](https://github.com/FlareZ123/workplace-v2/actions/runs/45053900884), [serialization](https://github.com/FlareZ123/workplace-v2/actions/runs/45054067890), [PDF annotations/forms](https://github.com/FlareZ123/workplace-v2/actions/runs/45054664915) and [MIME attachments](https://github.com/FlareZ123/workplace-v2/actions/runs/45055000534).
