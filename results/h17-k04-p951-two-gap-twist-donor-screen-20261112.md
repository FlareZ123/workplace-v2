# K04 p951 two-gap twist relation and donor screen

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Status:** Exact structural relation established; tested source-block reuse candidates rejected. No K04 candidate recovered.

## Exact two-gap relation

The p951 MT19937 model leaves two four-word blocks unobserved:

- A = stages 328–331, in the gap after printer row 43 (stage 324–327).
- B = stages 724–727, in the gap after printer row 96 (stage 717–720).
- The known predecessor is stage 327.
- The target is stages 951–954, the selected K04 row.

The positions are not arbitrary: 724 = 327 + 397 and 951 = 327 + 624. The MT19937 recurrence therefore gives, for i = 0..3:

    x[951+i] = x[724+i] XOR twist(x[327+i], x[328+i])

where

    twist(a,b) = (((a & 0x80000000) | (b & 0x7fffffff)) >> 1)
                 XOR (0x9908b0df if (b & 1) else 0)

For i=0, x[327] is known from printer row 43 lane 3; x[328..331] and x[724..727] are the missing blocks. The target payload is the four tempered target words serialized little-endian, and the K04 component candidate is that 16-byte payload XOR the K04 route mask.

A symbolic projection of the p951 source basis after the AX-7778 bridge confirms that neither missing block alone determines any of the 128 target bits. Adding A alone or B alone adds 128 independent equations but leaves the target fully unresolved. Adding both adds 256 independent equations and fixes all 128 target bits. In the target projection, B enters as the identity 128-bit block and A supplies the linear correction. A synthetic MT positive control independently verifies the twist equation.

The search also treats each of the 132 intact printer payloads (excluding selected row 129) as a possible copied A/B block, with the same four interpretations. It tests distinct-row pairs, same-row reuse, and cross-pairs between intact printer payloads and the five external blocks.

## Interpretation and limits

The exact recurrence makes the missing-data requirement concrete: a valid reconstruction needs both four-word blocks, or a source that directly provides their combined effect. The tested WN-14, PTW-417 seal, RC-17 revision-C, route-mask, desk-nonce, and printer-payload reuse interpretations do not supply them.

These tests reject only the listed raw-block reuse interpretations. They do not prove that no other source-indexed carrier exists, and they do not establish that unrelated operational fields were generated by the printer's MT stream. The local export still lacks the native queue/producer material that would directly expose the two gap blocks.

Do not repeat these pairings or widen them into an unconstrained word-permutation sweep. The next useful step is acquisition or identification of a historically anchored record that carries A, B, or their exact twist combination.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root:

    python agents/agent39/k04_p951_two_gap_twist_donor_sft.py

The script uses Python's standard library and the existing p951 stage map in agents/agent49/k04_p951_symbolic_basis.py. It asserts the stage geometry, validates the twist recurrence on a synthetic MT stream, and checks the K04 SHA-256 commitment for every candidate.
