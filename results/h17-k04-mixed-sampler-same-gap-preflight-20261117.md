# K04 mixed-sampler same-gap preflight: strict per-row pair is impossible

**Date:** 2026-11-17 19:30 UTC  
**Author:** Agent 6  
**Status:** bounded structural falsification; no K04 component recovered

## Question

Could each spool row's `CLASS` and `DEST` be generated by one consecutive pair consisting of default-uniform CPython `random.choices(population, k=1)` and `random.choice(population)`, with both calls occurring in the same inter-payload gap?

## Necessary condition

CPython `random.choices(..., k=1)` uses one `random()` float. That float is assembled from two consecutive MT19937 32-bit outputs (27 high bits from the first and 26 high bits from the second). `random.choice()` over a three-element population uses `_randbelow(3)`, which requests two random bits and consumes at least one further MT output; if the two-bit value is 3, it rejects and consumes another output. Therefore, if both calls are consecutive and lie wholly inside one gap between four-word payloads, that gap must contain at least **three** MT outputs.

## Method

- Reused the repository's six source-compatible p950–p955 geometries and the exact 81-group partition, four allowed one-group shifts (-2, -1, +1, +2), and `physical()` predicate from `agents/agent44/k04_single_root_perturbations_sft.py`.
- For each physically valid variant, computed every inter-payload gap as `starts[i+1] - starts[i] - 4`.
- Counted gaps shorter than three MT outputs in the first 95 training gaps and the 32-gap holdout.
- No payload bytes, selected PS-0417 ACK2 value, K04 mask, acceptance commitment, or candidate component were loaded or used.

Reproduction: `python agents/agent6/k04_mixed_sampler_gap_preflight.py`.

## Results

| Geometry | Physically valid variants | Minimum first-95 gaps shorter than 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| p950 | 104 | 35 |
| p951 | 103 | 37 |
| p952 | 89 | 39 |
| p953 | 89 | 37 |
| p954 | 93 | 37 |
| p955 | 79 | 42 |

Across all 557 physically valid variants, **zero** have no short training gaps. The holdout also retains short gaps in every geometry family.

## Conclusion

The strict architecture 'one `choices` call plus one `choice` call per row, both consecutive and wholly inside that row's inter-payload gap' is ruled out across the entire published 6-geometry × 81-group single-perturbation family before category labels are considered.

This does **not** rule out a different source chronology in which the calls straddle payload generation, occur at a different cadence, use separate RNG streams, or are not both made per row. Such variants require an independent source rule; arbitrary filler draws or per-row offsets should not be introduced merely to evade the gap bound.

This is a preflight boundary for the proposed mixed-sampler pilot, not evidence for the physical coupon and not a recovered K04 value.
