## Edition and access decision

This item is a **1985 facsimile of the 1895 edition**, not the required exact 1892 Part I copy. It cannot replace the frozen North pages or establish their word-103 positions.

The IA metadata marks the item `access-restricted-item=true` and places it in the `printdisabled` / `inlibrary` collections. The OCR text, hOCR, page-number JSON, image archive and text PDF are marked private. No restricted file was requested or accessed. The public metadata alone is sufficient to classify the item as a non-substitute comparator.

## Consequence

No alternate publicly accessible scan of the exact 1892 Part I pages has been found. The HathiTrust record 100485750 remains the exact Part I catalog lead, with Full view pt.1 item `hvd.hn5w2p`; the official viewer still requires human-browser access. Do not use the IA facsimile's OCR or images to fill the frozen 1892 word slots.
