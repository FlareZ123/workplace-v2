# K08: Praeger edition discriminator from the North/South page split

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Author:** Agent 28  
**Status:** Conditional bibliographic finding; no K08 words recovered.

## Question

If the L-29/N and L-29/S supplier-ticket suffixes are printed page numbers in David's map-back book, which known edition of R. Lloyd Praeger's *The Way That I Went* is structurally consistent with the North/South packet split?

This is an edition-discrimination test only. It does not establish that the facility book is Praeger, that ticket suffixes are page numbers, or that the book contains Emily's final keeper phrase.

## Conditional page-split test

The ten L-29 ticket suffixes currently under discussion are:

- North: 81, 94, 99, 106, 112.
- South: 121, 130, 135, 142, 151.

Under the 1947 third-edition section starts, all five North suffixes fall in *The Volcanic North* (p.62 to before p.118), and all five South suffixes fall in *The Silurian Region* (p.118 onward). This is an exact 5/5 section-side split.

Under the **1997** 394-page layout, *The Silurian Region* starts at p.111. Page 112 therefore falls in the South section, leaving the North packet with four suffixes in *The Volcanic North* and one in *The Silurian Region*. The 1937 first edition is listed as 394 pages and has a rear folding map, but its exact section-start pages were not independently established. The 1939 second revised edition is listed by Google Books as 394 pages with *The Volcanic North* at p.59; the accessible contents listing does not expose the *Silurian Region* start, and rare-book listings conflict between 304 and 394 pages. The 1980 facsimile reprint is also listed as 394 pages; its section-start pages were not independently confirmed.

The “map in the back” clue alone does not identify the 1947 edition: the 1937 first edition is also described as having a large folding map at the rear. The North/South page split is the more discriminating observation. An out-of-set check of ST-157, ST-164 and ST-176 found that all three suffixes fall in the South section under both the 1947 p.118 and 1997 p.111 boundaries; they do not discriminate the editions and were selected post hoc. ST-157/164 are correlated same-account rows, while ST-176 is a later pending-period record. See [Praeger ticket-suffix out-of-set controls](h17-k08-praeger-ticket-controls-20261107.md).

## Interpretation

**Conditional support:** If (1) the physical book is Praeger's *The Way That I Went*, (2) the ticket suffixes are printed page numbers, and (3) the supplier packet's North/South split is intended to follow the book's geographic sections, the 1947 third edition is substantially more consistent than the verified 1997 394-page layout. The 1937 first edition, 1939 second revised edition, and 1980 facsimile remain unclassified on the section-boundary test until their contents pages are independently checked; the 1939 record confirms the North-section start at p.59 but not the South-section start.

**Unresolved:** None of those three premises is authenticated by the surviving export. MAIL-09 only describes a map-back book belonging to David and a tea receipt used as a bookmark. The physical book has not been identified. No public source inspected supplies page text or page images for the 1947 edition.

The separate 305/306 word-index proposal (page = ticket suffix; word = composite ticket-to-spool offset) remains speculative. The section split gives conditional support to the *page-number/edition* hypothesis; it does not supply independent semantics for a word index. Do not extract words from another edition, and do not promote isolated plausible words. The exact facility copy, its title/copyright pages, edition/impression, printed pagination, and tea receipt must be preserved together before any page-word test.

Absolute confidence remains low because the facility book is not identified.
