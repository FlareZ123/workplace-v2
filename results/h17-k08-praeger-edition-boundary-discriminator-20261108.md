## Observed edition differences

The L-29/N ticket suffixes are 81, 94, 99, 106, 112. The L-29/S suffixes are 121, 130, 135, 142, 151. If these are page numbers in David's book, the edition's section boundary matters.

Public bibliographic sources expose a useful difference:

| Edition | Pagination | Relevant contents boundary | Physical description |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1937 first | 394 pages | Not established here | Blue cloth; large folding map at rear |
| 1939 second, revised | 394 pages | Not established here | Blue cloth; folded map at rear |
| 1947 third | 416 pages | The Volcanic North begins p.62; The Silurian Region begins p.118 | Blue cloth; folding map at rear |
| 1997 reprint | 394 pages | The Volcanic North begins p.59; The Silurian Region begins p.111 | 394-page reprint of the 1937 work |

The 1947 third-edition boundary at p.118 creates an exact 5/5 partition: every L-29/N suffix is below 118, and every L-29/S suffix is above 118. The 1997 reprint's p.111 boundary instead puts N-page 112 on the South side, producing a 4/1/5 partition. This makes the 1947 third edition the strongest *page-crosswalk* candidate if, and only if, the ticket suffixes are page numbers and the N/S labels are intended to track the book's two sections.

The 1937/1939 editions remain strong *physical-profile* candidates because catalogues describe blue cloth and a folding map at the rear. Their page-section boundaries have not been established here. Do not transfer the 1947 p.118 boundary to those editions.
