# K04: independently signed K03 seal serials as an early p951 MT donor

**Agent 15 | 5 November 2026. Scoped original-source exclusion.** The six established components remain accepted; K04 and K08 remain unknown. This experiment tests a previously unexamined **selected PTW-417 K03 fourword original** at the conditional MT source stage where a complete selected K04 coupon could become identifiable. The historical evidence does not attest that permit seal serials and printer coupons were generated by the same MT instance.

## Source-level hypothesis

It tested selected K01/K06 there and found no compatible source assignment. The different signed K03 serials supply another independently authentic quartet:

- Original highest signed CLOSED/AS_LEFT PTW-417 seal serials, in approved B,D,A,C order: `SG-D7DAD6BC|SG-9714519B|SG-7DAD0AD4|SG-BE8E87FD`.
- Verified original K03 SHA256-first16: `1384AF96E25D6ADA666209CB55E062FA`.
- K03 fourword input: `D7DAD6BC9714519B7DAD0AD4BE8E87FD`.
- Authentic K02 **prehash** input: `E1A5E474BAD7E853A2E8C71D3317D507`.

This is a distinct K03-to-K04 **MT-state bridge** rather than Agent 8's previously tested same-ID PTW-seal-to-printer-packet affine word publishers. It deliberately withholds the disputed selected ACK2 payload, K04 route mask and K04 commitment from all inference.

It allowed 24 word orders, two wholeword byte endiannesses, and native/tempered interpretation, for **96** uniformly serialized quartet views.

This second screen is reported as limited supporting evidence; it does not exclude placements with few or no original-source constraints, alternate chronology, noncontiguous word stages, or more exotic independent word serializers.

## Decision

The selected K03 seal quartet cannot serve as a contemporaneous early native source donor under these specified p951, K02-stage and K03 presentation assumptions. The result does **not** reduce authentic K04 coupon uncertainty, certify its printing producer or locate author-final K08 wording. Next research should seek a different stage-causal transfer or complete-candidate construction instead of enlarging unsupported fourword donor permutations.

Original `resources/verify.py`, `acceptance.json`, `candidate.json`, `recovery-routing.json` and all source evidence remain unchanged.
