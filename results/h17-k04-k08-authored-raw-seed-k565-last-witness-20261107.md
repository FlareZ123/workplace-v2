This is meaningful negative evidence for that particular attempted cross-slot mechanism, not evidence against all printable strings or arbitrary encoded/salted or other-length seeds. The remaining highest-value research should change the cross-slot **producer/capture mechanism** or find a distinct literal author-final carrier.
