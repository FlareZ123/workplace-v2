## Hypothesis and original source

A historical original ESC-N RECOVER attempt-2 physical coupon might have been issued by a compact cryptographic producer keyed by, or hashing, **two separate previously retained same-job print buffers**. Such a derivation is different from the published one-parent retry+metadata digest test (Agent7), pre-ACK multirow journal digest (Agent8), cross-slot HMAC with metadata messages (Agent18), and Boolean/AES/byte-level cache-collision mixing (Agent27). No original document states the printer actually used a two-parent KDF.

## Frozen full-candidate construction

Three distinct parent-pair cases: prior `LABEL1+REQUESTED1` (chronologically causal), `REQUESTED1+REQUESTED3` and `LABEL1+REQUESTED3` (18:03 is **after** selected event; these two are retrospective/copy hypotheses only). Each parent undergoes four source-defined complete 4-word endian/order presentations, independently. Ten fixed identifiers/salts: empty, K04, H17, ESC-N, full job id, RECOVER, ACK, attempt number, selected timestamp and archive incident id. Six parent/salt concatenation orderings.

For every resulting input, calculate the first and last 16 bytes of each digest `MD5, SHA1, SHA256, SHA384, SHA512, SHA3-256, BLAKE2b, BLAKE2s`; also calculate both HMAC directions using one parent as key and the other plus context as message, first and last digest16; SHAKE128 and SHAKE256 first16. Try full packet byte reversal. XOR each generated full 16-byte packet with the **original** mask, SHA256 it, and compare with the **original** untouched 32-byte target digest. Each parent-pair family has 96,000 candidate *assignments* (duplicates allowed).

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| LABEL1 + REQUESTED3 | 96,000 | 40,064 | 0 |
| **Total** | **288,000** | **120,192** | **0** |

An in-family planted first complete candidate is reobserved at six equivalent assignments. This confirms reduction/iteration coverage but is weaker than a blinded random source-generator control. The full original spool, route, and acceptance source SHA256 pins were asserted; original verifier and canonical candidate were never modified. Source-equivalent script executed locally from the supplied 46-member ZIP, confirming all counts. ## Research consequence

The complete original K04 component is absent from this **source-specific dual-input digest/HMAC grammar**, including the only strictly earlier source pair. This rejects a selected two-parent direct-hash issuer in those exact serializers and salts. It is not a general denial of cryptographic print producers, of unknown device secrets, of unrelated source-to-coupon mechanisms, or of historical copy damage. No selected physical coupon bit is authenticated, and no author-final K08 words are learned.

The strongest next discriminator is an **intact source packet generated by the exact candidate issuer** or a direct selected physical print artifact. Further enlarging already-negative arbitrary hash/salt menus without a producer-side witness would create little information.
