## Offensive research decisions: choose a *new discriminator*, then build a complete candidate

**K04, attempt-2 source-object identity:** Require each competing mechanism to name the original source field it interprets, the generation/dispatch/ACK-copy/media stage it predicts, and why the exact 18:02 RECOVER attempt-2 object is selected. The most valuable next demonstration is a genuinely comparable *intact original* withheld end-to-end, from which a producer or reconstruction rule predicts its **complete** selected object before reading the withheld value. Investigate a stage-selecting record relationship or different source representation if existing intact controls cannot discriminate. Alternate named PRNGs or more arbitrarily mutated bytes of the same ACK must present a new original discriminator.

**K08, final editorial/custody transition:** Start from who selected the *keeper* version and how that exact wording moved from draft/desk margin into an envelope, including document boundary, version, layout and quotation conventions. A proposed finite editor/extractor must make a distinctive, testable prediction about source-record structure or an independent original phrase. N17/N18 are historical **other-purpose** labels and retrospective test answers; recovering them only through hindsight vocabulary or weakly selective rankings is not validation of the keeper version. A frozen, source-conditioned complete-caption construction is worth an original commitment test, with shuffled metadata and masked-authentic controls whenever applicable.

**Cross-record relation or alternate recovery architecture:** Welcome any source-evidenced mechanism that connects an intact surviving record to the missing *complete* 16-byte component. State the exact proposed dependency and show why it is distinct from prior pairwise bytes, RS interpolation and source-splicing; predict at least one source-visible consequence before scaling. Do not assume the eight slots are independent merely because earlier generic cross-slot formulas failed.

**Decision standard:** Prioritize recovery probability and explanatory power over raw hashes evaluated, report count and procedural compliance. Economical falsifiers are useful; deep compute is warranted when a mechanism has a reason to touch the selected original. Synthetic planted positives check the *implementation*, whereas withheld genuine originals and shuffled-source controls test the *historical selection hypothesis*. This is guidance for experiment design, **not** a universal approval gate or a numeric cap; the maintainer explicitly authorizes creative and substantial attacks.
