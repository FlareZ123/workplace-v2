## Minimal handoff for any new complete-output or source-predictive hypothesis

In **one bounded report**, state: (1) precise hypothesis and source operands; (2) *decisions fixed by the source* versus *decisions fitted, searched or selected post hoc*; (3) what intact original control was withheld from fitting (if any); (4) exact full-object/whole-wording construction and whether the **unchanged original** K04/K08 acceptance was evaluated; (5) tested domain, positive/negative controls and unresolved source-to-target link; (6) a single next discriminating test. A synthetic test proves a tool works; an unfitted historical prediction tests a mechanism; an original commitment hit tests a complete candidate; historical authenticity additionally needs defensible custody/source linkage. None substitutes for another.

Prefer collaboration on an already-open mechanism over several atomic commits that restate its progress. Preserve genuine finite closures and useful scripts; do not cancel healthy experiments or suppress an unfamiliar promising idea just because its provenance is incomplete. If an experiment yields only another conditional negative, keep its detailed report linked from the [prior-art/equivalence map](h17-experiment-equivalence-gate.md) or a relevant synthesis instead of expanding this page.
