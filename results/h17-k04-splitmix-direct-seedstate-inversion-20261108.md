# K04: complete-source SplitMix expansion inverse of the MT initialized register

**Agent18, 8 November 2026.** Direct, invertible 32-bit and 64-bit SplitMix state-expansion models fail the complete historical four-word source gate. The recovered K04 coupon remains unknown. This test explores a distinct compact-state initialization mechanism, rather than appending a seed length or revisiting selected-copy bit damage.

## Original-source requirement and candidate-producing model

The independent [p951 scalar-initialization investigation](h17-k04-p951-scalar-init-genrand-624-phase-exclusion.md) derives four *complete* MT initial-register words using 132 original intact printer packets, with the selected 18:02 RECOVER ACK withheld:

```
S[2]   = E32E1557
S[3]   = 3152E7CB
S[4]   = BBDD46A0
S[622] = BBD5A4AB
```

The underlying spool is SHA256 `7d71d8bef7f01596e705ac14e24c5c4352042512b3088125afca40f3841b6083`. This is a **conditional** p951 MT-state reconstruction, not an independently authenticated firmware or selected-paper producer.

This experiment asks whether those 624 initial words were generated by a **single compact seeded expansion**, permitting direct recovery of the full 624-word register and therefore of all four native words of the withheld selected printer payload. Two real-world mathematically distinct expanders were considered:

- **64-bit SplitMix64:** one 64-bit output is divided into low/high or high/low 32-bit words, repeated 312 times to form a 624-word MT register. All **624 possible cyclic register rotations** are allowed in both orders. Whichever adjacent original pair S[2],S[3] or S[3],S[4] corresponds to a complete SplitMix64 output determines the candidate 64-bit initial seed **algebraically** by inverting the two odd output multipliers and XOR shifts. The other complete historical source words are withheld checks.
- **32-bit SplitMix32:** a native 32-bit SplitMix-style finalizer applied once per register word. Its odd multipliers and XOR shifts are invertible; an original S[2] gives one unique 32-bit seed for each of 624 cyclic register phases, which is then checked against complete S[3],S[4],S[622].

The inversions use modular inverses, not a restricted guessed seed domain. Neither model needs target SHA feedback or the damaged selected source payload to choose a seed.

SplitMix output inversion also passes original algebraic roundtrips at four extreme states. On the historical conditional state words, no seed/phase combination survives the fourth-word gate, so the recovery stage is not reached; **no K04 source bit is claimed**.
