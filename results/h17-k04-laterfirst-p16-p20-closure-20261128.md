## Distinct causal attack and novelty

Agent6's independently preserved later-first historical big-endian/AB run gave 414 terminal UNSAT and 610 UNKNOWN for original P12 cubes 0..1023 under a 5-second solver budget. Those 610 cubes remained unresolved, with no complete coupons generated. Agent7's P16 refinement had previously been used on the **forward** intact REQUESTED1 packet, but not against the later-first source view. This experiment applies source-free exact disjoint refinement to the *intact later REQUESTED3 alone*, reverses xoroshiro128++ to predict up to 18 earlier consecutive-output coupon windows and uses the unchanged complete original K04 acceptance commitment on every SAT state.

The new runner uses a direct one-transition full-width Boolean circuit (rather than constructing four unused future transitions). Every P12 cube comprises exactly 16 P16 subcubes, using additional first-state bits [4,5,6,7]. A P16 UNKNOWN is subdivided further into all 16 disjoint P20 subcubes, adding [8,9,10,11]. All recovered SAT models are blocked until terminal UNSAT or timeout; model cap/UNKNOWN remains explicitly open. This is an exhaustive *partition* of the initial-word space, **not** 20 guessed real source-state bits.

## Source-free controls

10,000 exact forward/inverse state round trips; an independent planted later packet recovers its complete state from its true P16 subcube, and all 16 P16 children are checked against a planted full 16-byte SHA-256 coupon commitment (positive hit). Five further independently planted P20 subcubes return terminal UNSAT after recovering the exact state. The solver is conditional on the xoroshiro128++ hypothesis and does not authenticate the printer.
